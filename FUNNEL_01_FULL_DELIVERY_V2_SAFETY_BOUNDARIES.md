# Funnel 01 — V2 Delivery Safety / Instructional Boundaries

Status: CURRENT RESEARCH BOUNDARY — apply to V2 design; clinical wording still requires Dott.ssa Mafalda Camposarcone review
Date: 2026-09-22

Purpose: preserve high-impact education/safety constraints discovered during the V2 redesign that affect materials and live delivery.

## 1. Cognitive aids / Family Emergency Pack

ERC 2025 education guidance supports cognitive aids in some training contexts but explicitly warns that bystanders should not use cognitive aids during resuscitation when doing so may delay critical actions.

Formalife design consequence:

- Emergency Action Cards / scenario cards / reference hub may be used for **preparation, study, rehearsal, refresh and post-course reference**;
- do not market them generically as something the parent should stop and read while performing time-critical CPR/choking actions;
- any real-time emergency-reference claim must receive specific scientific review;
- core time-critical actions must be trained for direct retrieval/action, not dependency on a card.

This does not make the Pack less valuable. It clarifies its job:

**prepare before -> rehearse after -> refresh over time**, while critical action itself should be trained to occur without unnecessary delay.

Source checked 2026-09-22:
- ERC Guidelines 2025 Executive Summary / Education for Resuscitation: https://www.erc.edu/science-research/guidelines/guidelines-2025/guidelines-2025-english

## 2. Attention threshold boundary

Do not state that adult attention biologically expires after 10 or 15 minutes.

Current literature does not support a universal fixed 10–15 minute attention limit.

Use an instructional design heuristic instead:

- avoid long passive monologues;
- change learner activity regularly;
- insert prediction, demonstration, practice, retrieval and feedback;
- manage fatigue with breaks/resets.

## 3. Feedback-device boundary

Current AHA/ERC resuscitation-education guidance supports real-time CPR feedback devices for training skill acquisition.

Formalife consequence:

- if feedback-capable manikins are economically feasible, treat them as a high-priority instructional equipment upgrade;
- if unavailable, instructor feedback remains necessary;
- do not claim superior real-world outcomes merely because feedback devices are used.

## 4. Spaced-learning boundary

A single live event should not be treated as sufficient for durable psychomotor retention.

Formalife already has one free practical refresh within 12 months. V2 additionally proposes light retrieval/booster contacts between the original course and refresh.

These contacts are educational reinforcement, not evidence of guaranteed long-term emergency performance.
