# Funnel 01 — Full Delivery System V1

Status: PROVISIONAL OPERATING DESIGN — translates founder-approved G2/G3/G4 into an executable product; exact clinical content and wording require Dott.ssa Mafalda Camposarcone review before customer use
Date: 2026-09-22

Purpose: make the rebuilt Full Course deliverable in a repeatable way before Formalife builds the high-intent sales surface. This is an operating/product-design document, not a clinical protocol.

Governing commercial promise:

**focused choking/weaning trigger -> broader parent-first preparedness system -> BEFORE -> LIVE -> AFTER / HOME REFERENCE + REFRESH.**

Governing product rule:

**live time is reserved for decision, demonstration, supervised practice, correction and human clarification; reference-heavy or lower-priority theory moves before/after the room.**

---

# 1. Decision boundary

## CURRENT FOUNDER DECISIONS used

- Full is broader than choking;
- choking/weaning remains the primary B2C acquisition wedge;
- Full is parent/caregiver education, not professional certification;
- approximately four meaningful live hours;
- maximum 12 live participants;
- 1 Caregiver EUR 80 / 2 Caregivers EUR 120;
- Guide included according to current G3 rules;
- Family Emergency Pack design direction approved;
- one free practical retraining opportunity within 12 months;
- dedicated customer communication stream;
- Total Protection / protected pre-enrolment / BTRF directions approved;
- current survival operating floor: 5 paying participants, while normalized target remains 10–12;
- scientific/medical material is reviewed by Dott.ssa Mafalda Camposarcone.

## PROVISIONAL elements in this file

Until founder/scientific approval or first implementation validates them:

- exact minute allocation;
- exact pre-course format;
- exact station choreography;
- precise Pack formats/counts;
- activation deadline/payment window;
- second-caregiver cutoff;
- refresh booking mechanics;
- exact feedback/proof-capture forms.

Do not silently promote these to clinical or commercial fact.

# 2. Edition state machine

Each Full edition should exist in one explicit state.

## `PLANNED`

Internal date/location hypothesis. Not customer-sellable yet.

Required to move forward:

- venue availability;
- instructor availability;
- curriculum/material version selected;
- commercial route selected: confirmed sale vs activation/pre-enrolment.

## `ACTIVATING`

Customer-facing status:

**EDIZIONE IN ATTIVAZIONE — PRE-ISCRIZIONE PROTETTA**

Current minimum:

**5 paying participants / committed pre-enrollees under the approved activation mechanism.**

Before customer commitment, publish:

- that the edition is not yet confirmed;
- minimum of 5 for that edition unless Formalife explicitly publishes another threshold before launch;
- confirmation deadline;
- exact Guide/refund/Training Credit mechanics.

### Recommended V1 operational deadline — HYPOTHESIS

Use a confirmation deadline **7 calendar days before the event** for the first tests.

Reason:

- gives customer reasonable planning certainty;
- aligns with the existing >7-day refund/transfer boundary;
- leaves time for logistics and final reminders;
- keeps activation campaigns from remaining uncertain until the last minute.

Test and revise if conversion/cash evidence says otherwise.

## `CONFIRMED`

The edition will run.

Required actions:

- activation customers receive confirmation immediately;
- any remaining balance is requested;
- direct sale becomes normal confirmed-edition checkout;
- onboarding/orientation begins;
- capacity counter becomes authoritative.

### Recommended balance window — HYPOTHESIS

For activation customers, request remaining Full balance within **72 hours of confirmation**. If unpaid after reminder/human check, release the Full seat while preserving the customer's already-earned Guide/Training Credit rights.

Do not confiscate Guide value because an activation customer does not complete the Full purchase.

## `CLOSED_FULL`

No more paid live seats available.

Route:

- waitlist / next edition;
- do not fabricate additional capacity;
- refresh requests cannot displace already-paid attendees.

## `DELIVERED`

Attendance and delivery checklist closed; proof/customer-success sequence begins.

## `FORMALIFE_CANCELLED`

Execute Total Protection:

- refund or priority transfer;
- EUR 20 goodwill credit under current approved direction;
- reason logged;
- customer communication immediate.

# 4. BEFORE — pre-course system

The pre-course layer should protect live time, reduce mismatch and increase readiness without becoming a second course.

## 4.1 Transactional confirmation

Immediately after confirmed purchase:

- date, start/end calendar window, location;
- participant formula: 1 or 2 Caregivers;
- names if needed operationally;
- what the Full is / is not;
- attendance-certificate boundary;
- what to bring / clothing or practical considerations if relevant;
- Total Protection summary + link to full terms;
- BTRF summary + link to terms;
- support contact;
- Guide/Pack fulfilment expectation.

## 4.2 Pre-course orientation asset

### Recommended size

**8–12 minutes total consumption.**

Format can be one short video plus concise page/email.

### Job

Not to teach emergency procedures remotely.

It should:

1. explain the parent-first preparedness objective;
2. frame `PREVENT -> RECOGNIZE -> ACT -> ESCALATE` as the course architecture, subject to approved wording;
3. explain why a large part of live time is practical;
4. set expectation that the Full is not a professional certification or individual medical consultation;
5. explain what the Guide and Family Emergency Pack are for;
6. tell participants how the free refresh works at a high level;
7. reduce logistical uncertainty.

## 4.3 One useful pre-course question

Optional, only if the answer is actually reviewed before delivery:

**“Qual è la situazione pediatrica che oggi ti preoccupa di più o che vorresti capire meglio durante il corso?”**

Use:

- instructor context;
- proof/market learning;
- not individualized diagnosis.

Do not add a long intake questionnaire before evidence shows it changes delivery or routing.

# 5. LIVE — recommended 240-minute meaningful allocation

This is an **instructional allocation hypothesis**, not a clinical protocol. Exact sequences, wording, actions and age distinctions require Mafalda's scientific sign-off.

The proposed 240 meaningful minutes deliberately keep more than half of the live attention on choking + unresponsive child/resuscitation + integrated practical decision work.

| Block | Minutes | Live job |
|---|---:|---|
| L0 — opening, boundaries, course operating framework | 15 | orient participant; explain parent-first job and decision logic |
| L1 — recognize urgency / escalation / common decision layer | 20 | establish reusable recognition/escalation framework without diagnosis |
| L2 — choking prevention, recognition, response + supervised practice | 65 | deepest signature block; demonstration, practice, correction, repetition |
| L3 — unresponsive child / resuscitation / AED awareness + supervised practice | 65 | meaningful hands-on essential introduction; link to choking/drowning context |
| L4 — breathing difficulty + severe allergic emergencies | 20 | recognition, first-response/escalation logic, approved device context where appropriate |
| L5 — seizures / febrile convulsions | 15 | recognition, what to do/not do, escalation logic |
| L6 — curated injury/exposure rapid-response framework | 20 | serious bleeding, burns, head injury/falls, poisoning, drowning context at first-action level |
| L7 — integrated scenarios + debrief + decision transfer | 20 | force recall across situations; reinforce framework and clarify boundaries |
| **TOTAL meaningful** | **240** | |

Add approximately **10–20 minutes break/transition** outside the 240-minute meaningful target.

Commercial calendar block remains approximately **4h10–4h20**.

### Why this allocation

- L2 + L3 alone = 130 minutes, >54% of meaningful live time;
- L7 can further reuse choking/resuscitation decisions;
- broader topics remain real but curated;
- the course does not grow by adding a new chapter every time a useful pediatric topic is discovered.

A new topic normally displaces existing minutes rather than extending the course.

# 6. Practical delivery choreography

## 6.1 Two-instructor operating model

Current normalized planning already assumes primary + secondary instructor.

Recommended V1 use:

- during explanation/demonstration, one leads and one observes/prepares stations;
- during major practical blocks, split participants into **two working groups of up to 6** where equipment/layout allow;
- instructors rotate or groups rotate according to the exercise;
- every participant should receive observable opportunity for practice/correction on the designated core skills.

This reduces variability and makes the 12-person cap operational rather than decorative.

## 6.2 Practice completion evidence

Use a simple instructor checklist for the core practical stations.

Purpose:

- confirm the promised practical component happened;
- identify delivery failures;
- improve instruction;
- produce process evidence for G4.

It is **not** a certification of clinical competence and must not be marketed as such.

## 6.3 Q&A boundary

Questions are useful but should not expand the course into individualized diagnosis or destroy the attention budget.

Route questions into:

- answer now — relevant, safe, within scope;
- reference later — useful but better in Pack/customer content;
- individual medical question — direct to appropriate clinician/medical pathway, not handled as generic course advice.

# 7. AFTER — Family Emergency Pack V1 build specification

Goal:

**make the broader preparedness system tangible and usable at home for approximately EUR 5 target physical cost per household/order.**

One complete pack per household/order; duplicate only small personal pieces where useful for a second caregiver.

## 7.1 Recommended physical architecture

### A. Compact A5 branded folder/envelope

Job: keep the system together rather than creating loose papers.

### B. Emergency Action Card set

Target: **5–7 small, durable cards**, exact medical content subject to Mafalda review.

Possible category architecture:

- emergency orientation / escalation framework;
- choking;
- unresponsive child / resuscitation reference;
- breathing / severe allergy;
- seizure;
- serious injury/exposure first-action reminders;
- index / “what belongs here vs when to seek professional care”.

Keep them quick-reference, not miniature textbook chapters.

### C. Fridge Emergency Card / Magnet

Contains only stable high-value fields:

- 112;
- blank family emergency contacts;
- blank child/caregiver fields the family chooses to complete;
- QR to current Formalife reference hub.

Avoid static local medical numbers or clinical instructions that are difficult to keep current unless specifically governed.

### D. Caregiver Handover Card

Family-completed template for grandparents/babysitters/other caregivers.

Possible non-diagnostic fields:

- parent/guardian contacts;
- emergency contact;
- address/location reference;
- child-specific information the family chooses to record;
- location of useful emergency materials;
- QR to approved reference resources.

Formalife should not become the data controller for unnecessary sensitive health data through this paper tool.

### E. Family Safety Checklist

Short prevention/readiness checklist tied to Full scope.

Not a room-by-room encyclopedia.

### F. Scenario Cards

Target: **4–6 short rehearsal prompts**.

Job:

- help two caregivers rehearse “what would we do first?”;
- reinforce decision structure;
- encourage household conversation.

Must state that they do not replace supervised manikin practice or professional certification.

### G. Digital Reference Hub access card

QR/link to a lightweight versioned page containing:

- current downloadable references;
- corrections/updates if necessary;
- course boundaries;
- refresh booking route;
- customer support/contact route.

Do not build a large digital library merely to justify the QR.

### H. 12-Month Practice Refresh Pass

Shows:

- entitlement;
- expiry logic;
- booking route;
- one-redemption rule;
- subject-to-capacity mechanic in clear customer-friendly language.

## 7.2 Cost gate

Target physical incremental pack cost remains approximately **EUR 5 per household/order**.

Get actual supplier quote before final print quantity.

If the pack materially exceeds target cost, first remove decorative production cost before removing high-use utility.

# 8. Customer-protection execution

Policies only differentiate Formalife if they are operationally easy to honor.

## 8.1 Total Protection workflow

Every reservation record should be able to enter one of:

- `ACTIVE_CONFIRMED`;
- `TRANSFERRED`;
- `REFUNDED_PRE7D`;
- `TRANSFERRED_7D_48H`;
- `TRANSFERRED_EMERGENCY_LT48H`;
- `NO_SHOW`;
- `NO_SHOW_RECOVERY_USED`;
- `FORMALIFE_CANCELLED_REFUND`;
- `FORMALIFE_CANCELLED_TRANSFER`.

Do not keep these cases as free-text email history only.

## 8.2 BTRF workflow

Customer request window: current approved direction = within 24 hours after completing Full.

Operational flow:

1. accept request through one clearly published contact/form;
2. ask optional learning reason, not as a hurdle;
3. mark `BTRF_REQUESTED`;
4. initiate approved refund promptly;
5. customer keeps Guide + Family Emergency Pack;
6. remove unused refresh entitlement;
7. suppress testimonial/referral asks while service issue/refund is active;
8. log amount, Stripe loss, materials retained and stated reason.

### Recommended service standard — HYPOTHESIS

Formalife initiates an approved refund within **2 business days** of a valid request. External bank/payment settlement time is outside Formalife control and should not be falsely promised.

## 8.3 EUR 20 Formalife-cancellation goodwill credit

Implementation still needs expiry/use terms.

Recommended V1 hypothesis:

- valid 12 months;
- non-cash;
- usable once against a future paid Formalife product/service;
- not stackable with another extraordinary goodwill credit unless manually approved.

# 9. Second-caregiver upgrade execution

Current commercial decision:

**1 Caregiver -> add second caregiver for +EUR 40, subject to real capacity.**

Recommended V1 operational rule — HYPOTHESIS:

- self-serve upgrade available until **48 hours before start** while at least one real paid seat remains;
- after cutoff, manual approval only if operations/equipment allow;
- never promise second caregiver when capacity is exhausted;
- record `SECOND_CAREGIVER_UPGRADE` event and source.

If capacity becomes routinely constrained, revisit price/mix before pushing this upgrade harder.

# 10. Refresh system

## 10.1 Entitlement

One practical refresh per participant within 12 months of original attendance.

Current format direction:

- join approximately final 90 minutes of a suitable Full edition;
- practical/decision rehearsal only;
- no repeat of the entire Full.

## 10.2 Capacity-protection rule — recommended V1

Do not reserve a permanently empty paid seat months in advance merely for refresh.

Use:

1. customer requests a preferred refresh edition;
2. request enters `REFRESH_WAITLIST`;
3. Formalife confirms the refresh place only when capacity is available under the edition's operating cap;
4. if repeated full editions prevent redemption before expiry despite timely customer requests, extend the entitlement until Formalife can offer a reasonable slot rather than allowing the benefit to expire because of Formalife capacity.

This keeps the promise customer-protective without silently sacrificing scarce paid capacity.

## 10.3 Track

- eligible participants;
- requested refresh date;
- confirmed refresh;
- attended refresh;
- no-show;
- days from original course;
- whether refresh occupied otherwise sellable capacity;
- short post-refresh usefulness feedback.

# 11. Proof-capture operating system

G4 defines proof strategy; this section makes it executable.

## 11.1 Pre-purchase / registration context

Capture only fields that change decisions or create useful evidence.

Minimum recommended set:

- source/partner/campaign;
- 1 or 2 Caregivers;
- trigger — optional concise choice/open field;
- already actively searching for a course? yes/no/unknown;
- Guide already owned? yes/no;
- consent states.

Do not turn checkout into market research.

## 11.2 At attendance

Edition record:

- participant/order ID;
- attended / no-show;
- curriculum version;
- Pack version;
- instructors;
- practical stations delivered;
- delivery deviation/service incident;
- photo/video permission if separately appropriate.

## 11.3 Immediate post-course feedback

Use a short form that all participants can answer.

Recommended core questions:

1. **Cosa ti ha portato a partecipare proprio adesso?**
2. **Qual è stata la parte concretamente più utile?**
3. **Entrando dal tema soffocamento/svezzamento, la parte più ampia sulle emergenze ti è sembrata coerente e utile oppure dispersiva? Perché?**
4. **Che cosa ti ha aggiunto la pratica supervisionata rispetto a leggere o guardare un contenuto?**
5. **Quale elemento pensi davvero di usare dopo il corso: Guida, Pack, framework, refresh, altro?**
6. **Che cosa è stato poco chiaro, debole o inutile?**
7. **C'era qualcosa che quasi ti aveva fatto non acquistare?**
8. **Se dovessi descrivere a un altro genitore che cosa rende questo corso diverso da come immaginavi un normale corso di primo soccorso/disostruzione, cosa diresti?**

Do not provide suggested testimonial wording.

## 11.4 Testimonial / review permission

Separate:

- internal feedback;
- permission to quote/use feedback;
- public review request.

Do not edit customer meaning to manufacture a stronger claim.

If there is an unresolved service problem, solve it before using that experience as promotional proof.

## 11.5 24-hour touchpoint

Jobs:

- surface service issue;
- enable BTRF claim;
- confirm digital reference access works;
- reinforce where Pack/Guide live.

Do not combine the guarantee reminder with pressure to leave a positive review.

# 15. Revision rules

Revise the delivery system if evidence shows any of these:

- broader scope consistently makes choking-led buyers feel the course is diluted;
- practical blocks receive materially less than intended time;
- two-instructor station design does not create enough individual correction;
- Pack is not used or understood;
- pre-course asset is ignored and does not protect live time;
- 4-hour meaningful duration causes material fatigue without corresponding value;
- Q&A repeatedly destroys timing;
- refresh promise becomes operationally difficult because paid capacity is full;
- BTRF/Total Protection generates material abuse or cost without conversion benefit;
- 2-Caregiver architecture damages scarce-seat economics more than it improves household value/conversion;
- 5-person survival editions consume founder capacity without producing enough cash to justify running them;
- medical/scientific review requires scope or wording changes.

# 16. First implementation sequence

Do not build every asset simultaneously.

Recommended order:

1. Mafalda review of the 240-minute curriculum allocation and clinical boundaries;
2. finalize live instructor/practice choreography;
3. build the minimal pre-course orientation;
4. prototype Family Emergency Pack within EUR 5 target;
5. implement customer protection / Training Credit / activation rules in checkout/admin;
6. build delivery + feedback/proof forms;
7. run first rebuilt Full edition;
8. postmortem the edition against G3/G4 and this delivery contract;
9. only then harden customer-facing sales copy and scale acquisition.

This sequence minimizes the risk of writing persuasive copy for a product experience Formalife cannot yet deliver consistently.
