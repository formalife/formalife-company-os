# Formalife Target Model

Status: CURRENT — founder-aligned target company architecture
Date: 2026-09-21

Purpose: define what Formalife is being built to become, separating the assets that already exist from the capabilities/products that must be created in the short term and the company architecture that belongs to the long term.

This is a target-state document. A component described in the short-term or long-term sections is not a claim that it exists today.

The primary international business reference is Tiny Hearts: a parent-first consumer company in pediatric safety/first aid that combines human authority, live and digital education, products, milestone expansion and a deliberate separation between consumer education and professional certification. Formalife's objective is not to imitate its branding, but to reach at least the same level of category ownership and company completeness in the Italian market, then extend it with additional proven mechanisms where they strengthen distribution, lifetime value or defensibility.

## 1. Target company

Formalife is being built as the Italian consumer company of reference for **pediatric safety, prevention and emergency preparedness for parents and caregivers**.

The company is organized around the real life of families rather than around training accreditation.

Formalife combines:

- recognizable human experts and scientific responsibility;
- live practical education;
- high-quality digital education;
- proprietary publishing;
- useful free resources and tools;
- physical products when they improve preparedness;
- specialist partnerships where an adjacent competence is better owned by another operator;
- healthcare, professional and community distribution;
- a commercial system that follows families through multiple safety needs and life stages.

The market-facing focus remains coherent: **safety, prevention and pediatric emergencies**. The company can build a broad catalog inside that territory without becoming a generic parenting platform.

## 2. Operating principles of the model

### Parent-first consumer education

The primary product is designed for the parent/caregiver who wants to be genuinely prepared, not for the certificate.

Curriculum, duration, format, practice, supporting materials and post-course experience are designed around learning value and family usefulness whenever regulation does not require otherwise.

### Human authority

Formalife is not an anonymous content platform. The people who teach, review and create the content are visible, attributable and credible.

Clinical/scientific responsibility is explicit. Specialist partners retain their real expertise and identity when that authority is part of the value delivered.

### Physical + digital + editorial

Formalife does not force every problem into the classroom.

Depending on the job to be done, the right product may be:

- a practical live course;
- an online course;
- a live digital session;
- a book or guide;
- a bundle;
- a reference tool;
- a physical kit;
- a hybrid path combining several of these.

### Relationship beyond one transaction

A family can enter Formalife from different points and continue to buy relevant products as the child grows and new risks or preparation needs appear.

The customer relationship is therefore designed around **life stage + safety need**, not around one isolated course date.

### Specialist partnership instead of unnecessary duplication

Formalife develops internally the competencies that belong to its core and combines with strong specialists when an adjacent vertical is already better owned elsewhere.

The intended result is a stronger Formalife offer and a stronger partner offer, not merely borrowed audience.

### Certification separated from consumer value

Professional/regulated certification and consumer education are different jobs.

Where certification imposes a fixed curriculum, Formalife keeps that product commercially and conceptually separate from proprietary consumer training. The certification track may share infrastructure and instructors, but it does not define what Formalife means to parents.

## 3. What Formalife already has today

Formalife is not starting from zero. The current asset base already contains several elements of the target model.

### Consumer products

- **Guida Anti-Panico al Soffocamento Pediatrico**, physical proprietary editorial product.
- **Corso Anti-Panico al Soffocamento Pediatrico**, practical in-person consumer course with maximum 12 participants and current Single/Couple architecture.
- **Serata Anti-Panico / Light**, pilot lower-friction format.
- **Credito Formazione**, which links Guide/Light purchases to the full course instead of treating each product as an isolated transaction.

### Publishing and intellectual property

Formalife has proprietary educational content, a publishing capability / publishing-house asset reported by the founder, the Guida corpus, teaching materials and the **Ferma. Valuta. Agisci.** educational structure.

Publishing is therefore a real strategic capability, not only a support activity for the current course.

### Human and scientific assets

Formalife already has real instructors, practical delivery experience and a pediatric/scientific-authority asset that can become part of a much stronger human-facing brand once current roles, credentials and customer-facing wording are fully reconciled.

### Delivery capability

Formalife has already delivered classroom education, supervised mannequin practice, small-group teaching and partner-hosted activities.

### Distribution

The company already has:

- a pediatrician relationship that has generated real B2C customers;
- referral/partner-hosted experience;
- a structured partner pipeline;
- access to family, educational, pediatric/perinatal and community ecosystems;
- a customer base from prior course/Guide transactions.

### Commercial and operating infrastructure

Formalife already has a public website, ecommerce/payment capability, tracking/attribution design, email/CRM-capable infrastructure, authentic course photography/proof and operating systems sufficient to support a much larger consumer business after technical and data reconciliation.

### Evidence from the first market wedge

Historical customers show a strong recurring concern around pediatric choking, with an estimated 50-60% of prior participants in the weaning/complementary-feeding phase.

This makes choking/weaning a credible initial entry point into the wider Formalife target model, without requiring the whole company to mean only "choking course".

## 4. Short-term company to build

The short-term objective is to turn the existing asset base into a recognizable **parent-first pediatric safety company**, before pursuing geographic scale or broad institutional expansion.

### 4.1 Brand and authority system

Formalife develops a public identity in which the company and its people are both visible.

The short-term brand system includes:

- clear public faces for scientific direction, teaching and specialist content;
- robust bios, credentials and role boundaries;
- consistent real-person photography/video;
- expert-led editorial and social content;
- recognizable Formalife educational standards across products;
- customer proof tied to real products and experiences.

The objective is to combine the scalability of a company brand with the trust of identifiable human experts.

### 4.2 Flagship: pediatric choking / emergency preparation

The current choking course remains the most natural initial flagship because it already has product, delivery, proof, materials and observed customer demand.

The flagship is developed as a consumer product with:

- strong live practical component;
- clear parent/caregiver framing;
- integration with the proprietary Guide;
- high-quality pre/post-course digital resources where useful;
- a designed path for couples / additional caregivers;
- refresh/reference mechanisms that make the value survive the course day;
- clinical correctness and explicit scientific governance;
- a customer experience sufficiently distinctive to support a strong brand rather than a commodity course comparison.

"Anti-Panico" and **Ferma. Valuta. Agisci.** remain potentially valuable product/didactic assets. They do not have to carry the entire market positioning by themselves.

### 4.3 Digital course portfolio

Formalife creates a serious digital education layer, not a collection of generic webinars.

The first portfolio is built inside pediatric safety/emergency needs and can include, as expertise and scientific governance become available:

- pediatric choking / first-aid preparation online;
- common pediatric emergency recognition/preparation modules;
- home and everyday safety education;
- structured refresh/revision products;
- specialist partner courses in adjacent areas that directly affect safety.

Where the content is suited to durable reference rather than time-limited certification, **lifetime access / long-lived access with updates** becomes a preferred product pattern.

Digital products are packaged both individually and in high-value bundles, so Formalife can serve families that cannot attend a live event and can increase customer value without adding classroom capacity at the same rate as revenue.

### 4.4 Publishing as a product engine

Formalife expands publishing from one Guide into a strategic product line.

The publishing system produces:

- proprietary Formalife books/guides;
- companion materials for courses;
- reference products designed for repeated use at home;
- co-created books with strong specialists in adjacent verticals.

The proposed collaboration logic with Svezzy fits this model: Formalife should not rebuild an already strong weaning competence from scratch. A book and/or digital course can combine Svezzy's specialist authority with Formalife's editorial, production and commercial system, with explicit co-branding/provenance and shared economics.

Svezzy is the current natural partnership candidate for weaning; any agreement remains subject to partner interest, economics, IP, data/customer ownership and contractual terms.

### 4.5 Free value and owned demand

Formalife develops free resources that are useful enough to be repeatedly consulted and shared, rather than treating free content only as promotion for the next course date.

The target is a growing library of high-intent safety resources/tools tied directly to parent problems and life stages.

The exact flagship utility still requires separate research. The governing pattern is the one proven internationally by companies such as Solid Starts: **solve a recurring problem for free -> earn repeated use and trust -> convert a portion of that relationship into paid education/products**.

This free layer is designed to create:

- search demand;
- social sharing;
- email/account acquisition;
- repeated return visits;
- first-party understanding of parent needs;
- natural bridges to relevant paid products.

### 4.6 Customer account and lifecycle CRM

The commercial system evolves from "person bought course X" to a parent/family relationship that knows, where lawful and useful:

- which products were purchased;
- relevant child life stage;
- courses completed;
- content/resources used;
- partner/source attribution;
- which safety need logically comes next.

This supports relevant follow-up, cross-sell, refresh and new-stage education instead of generic repeated promotions.

### 4.7 Distribution through trusted parent ecosystems

The current pediatrician channel becomes the first example of a broader distribution architecture.

Formalife develops repeatable relationships with:

- pediatricians and pediatric/perinatal professionals;
- birth/maternity and family structures;
- nurseries/preschools and parent communities;
- pharmacies and relevant family retailers;
- associations and local family networks;
- other trusted operators who already serve the same parents without directly replacing Formalife.

The long-term ambition is structural healthcare/family distribution similar in principle to the hospital/maternity network demonstrated by CPR Kids, not dependence on one individual relationship.

### 4.8 Two separate BLSD products

Formalife deliberately develops **two distinct BLSD offers**.

#### BLSD certificato

The certified product serves customers who need the credential.

Its curriculum, rules and certification pathway remain those required by the competent external/accreditation system. Formalife does not attempt to manufacture differentiation by altering what cannot legitimately be altered.

Its commercial job is clear: provide the required certified outcome professionally and reliably.

#### BLSD Formalife — non certificato

The proprietary consumer product serves people whose primary goal is **learning and preparedness rather than obtaining a credential**.

Its lack of certification is not hidden as a deficiency. It is part of the product logic: because it is not built to satisfy a certification syllabus, Formalife can design the experience around what is most useful for the participant.

The proprietary product can therefore use, subject to scientific validation:

- more deliberate practice;
- scenario work;
- individual feedback and repetition;
- home reference material;
- digital preparation and refresh;
- family/caregiver participation;
- a household sharing/refresh logic inspired by the **Learn -> Practice -> Share** pattern used by AHA Infant CPR Anytime;
- proprietary educational structures and a materially different customer experience.

The two BLSD offers must remain clearly distinguishable in purpose and communication:

**credential when the customer needs certification; proprietary Formalife learning experience when the customer wants to learn.**

This creates a potential category-level contrast in a market where certified BLSD training is highly standardized and comparable.

### 4.9 Commercial architecture and bundles

Formalife builds offers as a coherent system rather than a flat catalog.

A family may enter through:

- a free resource;
- a book;
- a low-friction event/Light product;
- a digital course;
- the flagship live course;
- a partner/sponsored initiative.

From there, the company presents the next relevant level of value.

Bundles can combine complementary courses/products at an economically compelling package price, following the direct-response discipline already visible in the strongest emerging Italian digital competitors, without turning Formalife into a generic parenting course marketplace.

### 4.10 Near-term economic objective

The short-term model must prove that Formalife can generate attractive economics from the **customer relationship**, not only from one classroom edition.

The operating scorecard must therefore evolve toward:

- first purchase conversion;
- contribution by product;
- customer acquisition cost by source;
- second-purchase rate;
- bundle/upsell acceptance;
- digital gross contribution;
- partner-sourced economics;
- revenue/contribution per customer over time;
- capacity required per euro of contribution.

The existing target of 10-12 paying participants per live course at EUR 80 Single / EUR 120 Couple remains an important operating target for the flagship, but it is no longer the entire business model.

## 5. Long-term company to build

Once the parent-first core, flagship economics and digital/publishing system work reliably, Formalife expands vertically inside the same market meaning.

### 5.1 Own the pediatric-safety vertical

Formalife progressively builds deep expertise/products across the safety problems parents repeatedly face as children grow.

Potential domains include, only when real specialist capability and scientific governance are available:

- choking and feeding-related safety;
- first aid and emergency response;
- home injury prevention / babyproofing;
- burns, falls, poisoning and ingestion risks;
- fever/convulsions and other high-anxiety pediatric situations where parent education is appropriate;
- travel/out-of-home safety;
- caregiver preparedness;
- age/stage-specific safety changes.

The reference logic is closer to Safe Beginnings/Tiny Hearts than to a general parenting academy: depth expands **inside the safety/preparedness territory**.

### 5.2 Formalife digital safety library

The digital layer becomes a large, maintained reference environment rather than a finite launch catalog.

The long-term product can include:

- substantial multi-lesson courses;
- lifetime-access reference courses where appropriate;
- searchable resources;
- updateable guides/checklists;
- expert live sessions;
- refresh modules;
- stage-based recommendations;
- customer progress/history.

The objective is that a parent can return to Formalife when the child changes stage or a new safety question appears, instead of rediscovering a different provider every time.

### 5.3 Physical product ecosystem

Formalife can add physical products when they reinforce the educational promise, such as:

- first-aid / medication organization products;
- reference cards/materials;
- family practice kits;
- safety products curated around verified use cases;
- course/book/product bundles.

Products are selected because they strengthen the safety system and customer relationship, not merely because they can be sold in ecommerce.

### 5.4 Specialist partner ecosystem

Formalife becomes capable of adding vertical expertise without becoming internally expert in every adjacent discipline.

Selected specialists can contribute:

- courses;
- books;
- tools;
- expert sessions;
- co-branded products.

Formalife contributes brand, publishing, production, distribution, marketing, ecommerce, customer relationship and cross-sell infrastructure.

This allows the company to broaden the value offered to the same parent while protecting specialist credibility and avoiding unnecessary duplication of already excellent external capabilities.

### 5.5 Third-party-funded access

After Formalife has substantial consumer proof and a measurable audience/outcome history, part of the education can be financed by organizations that benefit from serving parents.

Potential payers include:

- employers/welfare programs;
- insurers/health plans where applicable;
- pharmacies;
- healthcare/family structures;
- child/family brands;
- foundations/associations;
- other sponsors with legitimate alignment.

The parent remains the beneficiary while another party funds access. The Baby Academy/Walgreens model is an external reference for this mechanism, not a template for the exact Italian commercial structure.

### 5.6 Geographic scale and instructor network

Once flagship products are standardized and no longer founder-dependent, Formalife can expand beyond the current geography through a controlled instructor/location system.

The scalable system requires:

- standardized curriculum;
- instructor training;
- quality assurance;
- brand standards;
- operating manuals;
- central booking/data/marketing infrastructure;
- local delivery economics;
- audit and customer-feedback loops.

The final form may be an employed network, licensing, franchise or another structure; the strategic goal is the same: Formalife quality and commercial system must be transferable without requiring the founders to personally teach every class.

### 5.7 Healthcare distribution at scale

The long-term company has a repeatable healthcare distribution channel rather than isolated professional referrals.

Formalife aims to become an education partner that pediatric, maternity/perinatal and family-health ecosystems can repeatedly introduce to new parent cohorts.

This creates recurring access to customers entering predictable life-stage windows and reduces dependence on paid media alone.

### 5.8 Publishing platform

The publishing capability grows into a meaningful Formalife business asset:

- proprietary safety titles;
- expert co-authored books;
- course companion publications;
- partner-distributed editions;
- books that act both as profitable products and durable acquisition/authority assets.

Publishing can therefore generate revenue, authority, distribution and customer acquisition simultaneously.

## 6. Target customer journey

The mature Formalife relationship is not one fixed funnel. It is a connected system with multiple legitimate entry points.

A typical path can look like:

**free useful resource / partner introduction -> book or digital entry product -> flagship education -> complementary bundle / additional caregiver -> refresh or next life-stage need -> physical/reference products -> referral / partner ecosystem.**

Other customers may enter directly through a live course, certified BLSD, a partner-funded initiative or a specialist collaboration and then join the same customer relationship.

The system therefore compounds value through:

**owned demand -> first transaction -> real customer experience -> second relevant purchase -> life-stage reactivation -> referral -> partner distribution.**

## 7. Brand architecture

Formalife remains the central consumer company/brand for the pediatric safety and preparedness territory while that meaning remains coherent.

Individual products can have strong product names and proprietary educational concepts, but no product name is required to carry the entire meaning of the company.

**Anti-Panico** is therefore an asset within the portfolio, especially where fear/confusion is relevant, rather than an obligation to define every future product.

When a future activity serves a materially different category, buyer or meaning, its brand architecture must be evaluated separately instead of automatically stretching Formalife across unrelated parenting categories.

The certified-training track is functionally separated from the consumer proposition so that accreditation never forces the main consumer brand to become a certification factory.

## 8. Competitive posture in Italy

Formalife does not need to beat every Italian competitor at its own specialty.

The intended advantage is the **integrated company system**:

- parent-first human brand;
- serious practical live products;
- high-quality digital catalog;
- publishing capability;
- specialist partnerships;
- durable free utilities;
- customer lifecycle/data system;
- healthcare and partner distribution;
- physical products where useful;
- separate proprietary and certified education tracks;
- progressive vertical expansion inside pediatric safety.

An Italian competitor may be stronger at one course, one app or one specialist topic. Formalife's target is to become difficult to compare because the customer enters a complete, credible and human pediatric-safety ecosystem.

`Genitori.app` should be watched as an important Italian digital/commercial benchmark for bundle architecture, direct-response execution and lifecycle breadth, but Formalife's target meaning remains narrower and more ownable: **safety, prevention and pediatric emergencies**, supported by identifiable experts and real physical/practical delivery.

## 9. Sequence of construction

The target model is built in causal order.

### Current base

**Guide + full choking course + Light pilot + Training Credit + instructors/scientific assets + publishing capability + partner distribution + existing customer/proof base.**

### Short-term build

**human authority system + strengthened flagship + digital safety catalog + publishing line + recurring free-value engine + lifecycle CRM/account + specialist partnership model + two-track BLSD architecture + repeatable professional/healthcare distribution + bundle/cross-sell economics.**

### Long-term build

**deep pediatric-safety vertical + large digital reference library + physical product ecosystem + specialist partner network + third-party-funded access + geographic instructor network + structural healthcare distribution + scaled publishing platform.**

## 10. Governing strategic decision

Formalife is no longer being designed as a company whose business model is "fill courses".

The target is a **parent-first pediatric safety consumer company** whose live courses are flagship products inside a larger system of education, publishing, digital products, tools, partnerships, distribution and customer lifetime value.

Tiny Hearts is the clearest primary international reference for the type of company Formalife intends to become. Formalife's ambition is to reproduce that level of category focus and company completeness in Italy, while adding mechanisms that make the Italian model stronger where they are economically and operationally justified.
