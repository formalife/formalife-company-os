Status: CURRENT PRODUCTION PROPOSAL — preserves founder-approved Pack architecture; physical implementation and supplier economics not yet founder-approved
Date: 2026-09-25

Purpose: convert the approved Family Emergency Pack direction into a small, manufacturable, versionable and quotable household preparedness kit without turning it into a decorative bonus stack.

---

# 1. Product job

The Pack is not a pile of gifts.

Its job is to help move selected learning from the live event into the household through:

1. quick reference;
2. family/caregiver handoff;
3. one concrete household-safety action system;
4. low-stakes retrieval practice;
5. access to current/versioned resources;
6. refresh entitlement;
7. facilitated referral.

The Pack must be useful even if the customer never assigns a fictional retail value to any component.

---

# 2. Household rule

Current founder-approved architecture:

- **one Pack per household/order**;
- do not give two complete Packs merely because two caregivers attend;
- duplicate only small personal pieces where the second caregiver materially benefits;
- 2 Pass Sicurezza per household/order remain mandatory under the referral architecture.

This protects both household coherence and 2-Caregiver economics.

---

# 3. Proposed V1 physical architecture

Use standard print sizes and a restrained number of pieces to keep assembly, replacement and supplier quoting simple.

## P1 — Emergency Action Cards — 4 cards

### Format proposal

- A6;
- double-sided;
- 350–400 gsm card stock;
- matte lamination or equivalent wipe-resistant finish only if supplier economics permit;
- rounded corners optional, not required.

### Working card set

**EAC-01 — Soffocamento, bambino >1 anno**  
Decision/action quick reference.

**EAC-02 — Soffocamento, lattante <1 anno**  
Decision/action quick reference.

**EAC-03 — Non risponde / non respira normalmente: RCP + DAE**  
High-level sequence/reference; not a substitute for training.

**EAC-04 — Altre emergenze: mappa della prima azione / escalation**  
One-line orientation across the selected ACT 5 categories. This card must remain highly compressed and is removed/reworked if final clinical QA shows that compression creates unsafe ambiguity.

### Design rule

Front = recognition / first decision.  
Back = action / `NON FARE` / escalation anchors.

Do not reproduce course slides or full procedures.

---

## P2 — Fridge Emergency Card / Magnet — 1 household piece

### Job

Put stable household emergency information where another caregiver can find it quickly.

### Format proposal

A6 portrait or approximately equivalent refrigerator format.

Supplier should quote two implementations:

**Option A — full magnetic sheet**  
Higher perceived permanence, potentially higher cost.

**Option B — printed card + adhesive magnetic strip**  
Potentially lower cost while preserving refrigerator use.

### Fields

- `112` prominent;
- household address / useful location details — blank write-in field;
- parent/caregiver contacts — blank fields;
- pediatrician / relevant family medical contact — optional blank field;
- QR to current Formalife reference hub;
- material/version date.

Do not embed volatile long-form medical guidance on the magnet.

---

## P3 — Caregiver Handover Card — 1 household piece

### Job

Help parents transfer essential preparedness information to grandparents, babysitters and other regular caregivers.

### Format proposal

A6 double-sided, 300–350 gsm.

### Structure

Front:

- child's name / household reference;
- parent contacts;
- address/location;
- pediatrician/contact if family wants it;
- known household-specific notes written by the family only where appropriate.

Back:

- where the Formalife quick references are kept;
- where the Family Emergency Pack is kept;
- reminder to call 112 for emergency situations rather than improvising;
- QR/reference-hub access.

Do not collect or send these handwritten household fields back to Formalife by default.

---

## P4 — Family Safety Checklist — 1 household booklet/sheet

### Format proposal

A4 sheet folded to A5, 4-panel / 4-page equivalent; heavier uncoated stock suitable for writing.

### Jobs

- convert the course into specific household actions;
- capture the ACT 7 `entro domani` commitment;
- identify who else regularly cares for the child;
- decide where emergency contacts/reference materials live;
- record whether both relevant caregivers know where the Pack is;
- note refresh entitlement / target revisit month;
- provide a simple periodic household check, not a generic babyproofing encyclopedia.

### Suggested sections

1. `ENTRO DOMANI` — one concrete action;
2. `CHI SI OCCUPA DEL BAMBINO?` — family/caregiver map;
3. `DOVE SONO I RIFERIMENTI?` — Pack / contacts / emergency information;
4. `COSA VA CONTROLLATO?` — small selected safety checklist;
5. `QUANDO RIPASSIAMO?` — refresh / household review reminder.

---

## P5 — At-home Decision / Scenario Cards — 4 cards

### Job

Create short retrieval prompts at home without pretending that card practice replaces manikin practice.

### Format proposal

A7 or approximately 74 × 105 mm;
- double-sided;
- 300–350 gsm;
- no lamination required unless quote difference is negligible.

### Grammar

Front:

- one scenario;
- maximum 3–4 facts;
- no answer cue.

Back:

- `FERMA -> VALUTA -> AGISCI` orientation;
- one correction/anchor;
- explicit `non sostituisce la pratica su manichino` micro-line where useful.

### Proposed V1 scenario mix

Use four different cognitive jobs, not four choking variants:

1. effective -> ineffective cough;
2. unresponsive child / CPR entry;
3. button battery / high-risk ingestion;
4. one rotating ACT 5 scenario selected for the edition/version.

Final clinical wording remains subject to QA.

---

## P6 — Access + Refresh Card — 1 household piece

Combine two approved Pack functions into one physical card without removing either customer entitlement.

### Format proposal

A6 double-sided or DL-size compact card.

### Front — current resources

- QR / short URL to Formalife versioned reference hub;
- support/access instruction;
- visible material version.

### Back — 12-Month Practice Refresh

- state the one-free-refresh entitlement at a high level;
- explain that booking is required and subject to the final operating rules;
- QR/URL to the refresh booking/status path when production-ready;
- entitlement itself must also exist in customer records; the physical card is not the only proof of eligibility.

This avoids creating a fragile standalone paper token that can be lost.

---

## P7 — Personal Quick Reference Card — 1 per attendee

This is the only standard duplicated personal piece for a 2-Caregiver order in the proposed V1.

### Format proposal

Wallet/business-card size, approximately 85 × 55 mm, double-sided.

### Job

Carry the smallest memory anchors from the course.

### Working content direction

Front:

`FERMA -> VALUTA -> AGISCI`

Back:

- choking observable-state anchor;
- pediatric CPR high-level memory anchor;
- 112 / professional-help cue.

Final wording must survive clinical QA and readability testing. If the information cannot remain safe and legible at wallet size, remove this component rather than shrinking text below useful size.

Quantity:

- 1-Caregiver order -> 1;
- 2-Caregiver order -> 2.

---

## P8 — Pass Sicurezza — 2 per household/order

Founder-approved referral component.

### Format proposal

Business-card / A7 compact referral card.

### Must include

- simple recipient relevance statement;
- QR/short link to the two-way referral router;
- referrer attribution code;
- no request for the friend's contact details to be written on the card.

### Cost-control implementation proposal

Use a standard printed Pass plus a small unique QR/code label generated/applied per customer rather than requiring expensive variable-data commercial printing in V1, unless supplier quote shows negligible difference.

The attribution mechanism must remain reconstructable end to end.

---

## P9 — Outer folder / envelope — 1 per household/order

### Job

Keep the system together at home without pretending the packaging itself is premium value.

### Format proposal

C5/A5-compatible branded document envelope, slim folder or paperboard sleeve.

Requirements:

- fits folded A5 checklist + A6 pieces;
- opens/closes repeatedly;
- no rigid gift box;
- no foam insert;
- no premium packaging that pushes economics away from the approximately EUR 5 planning target without customer evidence.

# 4. Proposed physical count

## 1-Caregiver order

- 4 Emergency Action Cards;
- 1 Fridge Emergency Card/Magnet;
- 1 Caregiver Handover Card;
- 1 Family Safety Checklist;
- 4 Scenario Cards;
- 1 Access + Refresh Card;
- 1 Personal Quick Reference Card;
- 2 Pass Sicurezza;
- 1 outer folder/envelope.

Total individual printed pieces: **15 + outer folder/envelope**.

## 2-Caregiver order

Same household Pack plus:

- **1 additional Personal Quick Reference Card only** as the default V1 duplicate.

Do not duplicate the full Pack or all Action Cards by default.

---

# 5. Version-control system

Every medically relevant printed piece must show a compact version marker, for example:

`Formalife FEP V1.0 — 2026-09`

Operational rules:

- version is recorded in the edition delivery record;
- current reference hub can be updated without reprinting every old QR if the URL remains stable;
- material changes to clinical instructions require a new print version;
- obsolete clinical stock must not be mixed silently with current stock;
- minor graphic/reprint corrections that do not change meaning may use a minor version.

---

# 6. Production / assembly rule

V1 should be hand-assemblable at low volume.

Suggested sequence:

1. print generic components in batches;
2. add/refill unique referral QR/code labels;
3. add edition/current version pieces;
4. include correct number of personal cards by attendee count;
5. quality-check component count;
6. place in outer folder;
7. hand out one Pack per household/order during ACT 7.

Do not create a fulfilment process requiring bespoke professional kitting for the first editions unless supplier economics justify it.

---

# 7. Supplier quote brief

Request price tiers at minimum for:

- 25 Packs;
- 50 Packs;
- 100 Packs;
- 250 Packs.

Supplier should separately price:

1. A6 laminated/wipe-resistant cards;
2. A6 standard 350 gsm cards;
3. A7 cards;
4. A4-to-A5 folded writing sheet/booklet;
5. wallet cards;
6. full magnetic A6 vs card + magnetic strip;
7. C5/A5 folder/envelope;
8. optional rounded corners;
9. collating/kitting vs loose delivery.

Quote must distinguish VAT/shipping where relevant.

Do not accept one aggregate quote that prevents Formalife from identifying the expensive component.

---

# 8. Economic gate

Current founder planning estimate:

**approximately EUR 5 per household Pack.**

This remains an estimate, not a fact.

Decision rule after quotes:

1. preserve learning/household function first;
2. remove decorative finish before removing useful function;
3. combine physical pieces where this does not harm usability;
4. compare magnet implementation alternatives;
5. avoid duplicating household pieces for second caregiver;
6. if V1 still materially exceeds EUR 5, recalculate Full contribution before approving the higher spec.

Do not hide Pack cost inside vague course overhead.

---

# 9. Proof / measurement plan

The first rebuilt Full editions must test whether the Pack is actually used.

Capture selectively:

- did the customer take/understand the Pack handoff?;
- which component they expect to use;
- later whether Guide/Pack/reference hub was consulted;
- whether another caregiver used/shared a component;
- whether the refresh card produced a booking;
- whether Pass Sicurezza produced attributed referral traffic;
- replacement/reprint problems;
- components ignored or misunderstood.

A nice-looking Pack is not proof of customer value.

# 11. Revision / kill conditions

Revise the proposed Pack if:

- supplier economics materially damage Full contribution;
- the number of pieces creates confusing handoff or low usage;
- final clinical QA makes a quick-reference object unsafe or misleading;
- first customers do not use/understand a component;
- digital reference can perform a job materially better without weakening household access;
- a component exists mainly because it looks like a bonus rather than serving a preparedness job.

Do not expand V1 with merchandise, gadgets, first-aid products or sponsor items without separate customer/economic evidence.
