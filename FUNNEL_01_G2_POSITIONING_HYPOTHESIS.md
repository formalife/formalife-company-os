# Funnel 01 — G2 Operational Difference / Positioning Hypothesis

Status: PROVISIONAL POSITIONING HYPOTHESIS — not yet a founder-approved market claim; must be earned operationally and tested
Date: 2026-09-22

Purpose: advance Gate G2 of `B2C_FUNNEL_01_CHOKING_FULL_ADAPTIVE.md` without inventing a verbal USP. This document defines the strongest current positioning hypothesis for Funnel 01, the operational commitments required to make it true, the contrasts it should and should not use, and the prospective evidence required before treating the position as validated.

This document does not supersede `FORMALIFE_TARGET_MODEL.md`. The target model defines what Formalife is being built to become. This file defines how the first B2C commercial engine may make that strategy concrete for a specific initial customer and buying moment.

---

## 1. State of evidence

### FORMALIFE EVIDENCE

Current evidence supports:

- pediatric choking concern was common among historical B2C participants;
- approximately 50–60% of prior participants were estimated to be in complementary feeding / weaning;
- the pediatrician channel transferred enough trust to generate most historical participants;
- Formalife already owns a proprietary choking Guide, practical-delivery capability, Single/Couple architecture, customer proof and a broader parent-first pediatric-safety target model.

### OPEN

Formalife does not yet know reliably:

- the active-demand versus latent-demand mix;
- whether the complementary-feeding cohort is the economically best scalable beachhead;
- which alternative a future prospect would otherwise choose;
- which Formalife difference customers will actually perceive and value enough to pay for;
- the CAC/payback of cold B2C acquisition.

Therefore the output of G2 must remain a **testable position**, not a declaration of category leadership.

---

## 2. Competitive reality that constrains the position

The Italian market already contains operators offering many features that cannot be treated as Formalife differentiation by themselves:

- theory + hands-on practice;
- mannequins and individual practice;
- choking + pediatric CPR;
- broader pediatric first-aid topics;
- small groups;
- healthcare/medical authority;
- parent/caregiver framing;
- weaning / safe-cutting integration;
- manuals and post-course support;
- repetition / refresh;
- couple/family participation;
- certification or attendance credentials;
- promises of confidence, calm or readiness.

Current external checks reinforce this. For example, Camillo Rescue Baby 2.0 currently communicates individual computerized mannequins, repeated exercises, a standardized method, a manual, continuing support and free repetition within 12 months. Other current Italian offers provide theory + practice and broad pediatric-emergency coverage at materially lower prices than Formalife's current EUR 80 Single / EUR 120 Couple architecture.

Implication:

**Formalife cannot win G2 by stacking ordinary course features or by claiming generic superiority.**

---

## 3. Positioning problem to solve

The initial buyer does not need another abstract claim that Formalife is “the best pediatric first-aid course”.

The position must answer:

**Why should a parent/caregiver entering a high-salience child-safety transition choose Formalife instead of a low-cost local course, a medically branded provider, a national practical-training operator, free information, or doing nothing for now?**

The answer must be visible in the product and customer experience before it is placed in copy.

---

## 4. Recommended positioning stack — HYPOTHESIS

### 4.1 Category Formalife intends to own

**Parent-first pediatric safety, prevention and emergency preparedness.**

This is broader than “disostruzione pediatrica” and narrower than generic parenting or pediatric medicine.

### 4.2 Initial commercial beachhead

**Parents/caregivers entering or currently in complementary feeding who feel a material responsibility gap around choking and pediatric emergencies.**

The market-facing entry remains specific even though the Full product is broader.

### 4.3 Core customer job

Not:

**“take a four-hour first-aid course.”**

Working job:

**“I want my household to be meaningfully better prepared to recognize and handle the first critical minutes of the pediatric emergencies I am realistically most likely to face, without having to become a healthcare professional or chase fragmented information.”**

### 4.4 Main contrast

The strongest current contrast is not “Formalife teaches the maneuver better”.

It is:

**isolated course / fragmented information / credential-led syllabus**

versus

**a parent-first preparedness system designed around the real decisions, practical actions, reference needs and caregiver context of a family.**

This contrast is a HYPOTHESIS until customer response proves it matters.

### 4.5 Product-level promise territory

Subject to G0 scientific validation, the Full should be designed to help a lay parent/caregiver become better prepared to:

**PREVENT what is realistically preventable -> RECOGNIZE when something is seriously wrong -> ACT with the correct immediate first response -> ESCALATE to appropriate professional help.**

This is a decision-and-action framework across selected high-consequence situations, not an encyclopedic medical curriculum.

### 4.6 Working market-facing concept

Internal positioning concept, not approved final copy:

**Formalife prepares families for pediatric emergencies as families — not for a certificate and not for one isolated maneuver.**

Alternative internal formulation:

**From the first high-salience risk to broader household preparedness.**

These formulations are design constraints, not slogans ready for advertising.

## 5. Operational commitments required to earn the position

The position is invalid if Formalife merely rewrites the sales page. To deserve the contrast, the Full system must progressively make the following true.

### A. Parent-first curriculum architecture

- live time prioritized by parent consequence and value of supervised practice;
- no attempt to imitate a professional-certification syllabus when certification is not the customer job;
- explicit exclusions so the course does not pretend to be pediatric medicine.

### B. One reusable emergency decision framework

The course must give the participant a coherent way to orient themselves across different events rather than memorizing an unrelated list of topics.

`Ferma. Valuta. Agisci.` may support the didactic structure but must not be presented as a proprietary clinical protocol or proof of superior outcomes.

### C. Practical depth where physical performance matters

Choking and the unresponsive-child / resuscitation introduction must receive meaningful supervised practice, correction and repetition.

Formalife must not claim superiority merely because practice exists; the difference is how practical work is integrated into the preparedness system.

### D. Durable home reference

The customer should leave with an approved reference layer that extends usefulness beyond the classroom.

The existing Guide is an asset, but the broader Full may require additional post-course reference architecture because a choking-only Guide cannot by itself support every Full topic.

### E. Household / caregiver logic

Single/Couple and future caregiver-extension mechanisms should reflect the reality that pediatric emergencies happen inside a care network.

The Full should make it easy to decide who in the real household/care network should be prepared, without forcing every customer into Couple.

### F. Scientific responsibility visible and bounded

- named, verified scientific responsibility;
- transparent roles and credentials;
- clinical claims versioned and approved;
- clear boundary between education and medical advice;
- update responsibility defined.

This is part of the operating system, not a decorative badge.

### G. Before / during / after customer experience

The Full should eventually be experienced as a system rather than a four-hour room rental:

**appropriate pre-course orientation -> live supervised learning -> home reference -> post-course support/refresh path when justified.**

Not every layer needs to exist in V1, but each added layer must have a clear customer and economic job.

---

## 6. Deliberate trade-offs

A position becomes more credible when it says what Formalife is not optimized for.

Candidate truthful trade-offs, subject to final product design:

- **not the cheapest course** — if Formalife charges more, the package must visibly contain more relevant value;
- **not a professional certification product** — the Full is optimized for parent/caregiver preparedness;
- **not an encyclopedic pediatric-medicine class** — live time is curated around high-consequence recognition, first response and practice;
- **not only a choking class** — choking is the entry wedge and a deep practical block, but the Full is broader;
- **not passive content consumption** — where motor skill matters, supervised practice remains required.

Do not publish a trade-off before it is true and before its commercial effect is tested.

---

## 7. What Formalife must NOT claim as the difference

Do not position the Full primarily around any one of the following:

- “we use mannequins”;
- “we do practice”;
- “small groups”;
- “our instructors are qualified”;
- “we teach prevention”;
- “we teach choking and CPR”;
- “we include a manual”;
- “parents can attend as a couple”;
- “you will stay calm”;
- “our method makes you remember under stress” without evidence;
- “scientifically better” without comparative evidence;
- “unique in Italy” without a defensible market basis.

These may be offer/proof components. They are not sufficient positioning.

---

## 8. Offer consequence of this positioning

G3 should not build the Full offer as:

**4 hours + topics + mannequins + certificate + price.**

It should build a visible preparedness package around the job above.

The future G3 Offer Card must show at minimum:

- who the Full is specifically for;
- what preparedness gap it solves;
- how choking-specific demand bridges to broader preparedness;
- what the customer receives before/during/after;
- what live practice is prioritized and why;
- what durable reference remains after the event;
- Single/Couple/caregiver logic;
- verified human/scientific responsibility;
- what is explicitly outside scope;
- risk reducers;
- price and terms;
- proof matched to each important claim;
- real capacity/date reason to act.

## 9. Prospective test plan for G2

Do not attempt to prove the entire position with broad paid scaling.

### Test population

Start with the nearest reachable demand:

- existing relevant leads;
- Guide buyers;
- partner/pediatrician referred parents;
- high-intent search traffic once instrumentation is reliable.

### Capture at entry / sale / post-delivery

Where appropriate and with minimal friction, capture:

1. real trigger;
2. prior solution / alternative considered;
3. why Formalife was considered;
4. which part of the offer created the strongest reason to choose;
5. whether the broader preparedness meaning was understood from a choking-led entry;
6. actual no-sale barrier;
7. after delivery, what the participant perceived as materially different and useful.

### Primary evidence

G2 becomes stronger when customers independently repeat concepts materially close to:

- “not only choking”;
- “prepared for what to do in the first minutes”;
- “clear decision framework”;
- “practical + something I can use afterwards”;
- “designed for us as parents/caregivers rather than for a certificate”;
- “worth choosing despite lower-cost alternatives”.

Do not coach customers to repeat these phrases. Their spontaneous language is evidence.

### Economic evidence

Read by route/cohort:

- Full conversion;
- complete CAC;
- normalized contribution;
- Single/Couple mix;
- no-sale reasons;
- price-comparison incidence;
- time to purchase;
- referral/proof creation after delivery.

A positioning hypothesis that customers praise but that cannot support sustainable acquisition economics does not pass G2.

---

## 10. G2 pass / fail criteria

### PASS ENOUGH TO ADVANCE

G2 can be treated as provisionally passed when:

1. the promised difference is operationally true in the Full experience;
2. customer-facing wording survives G0 scientific/credential review;
3. a meaningful share of real customers can articulate the difference without being prompted with the company's exact language;
4. the offer is not reduced primarily to price comparison against generic courses;
5. conversion and contribution are compatible with the chosen route;
6. the broader preparedness meaning is understood despite choking-led acquisition.

No universal numeric thresholds are invented here. Initial thresholds belong in the first controlled commercial test once baseline volume is known.

### FAIL / REVISE

Revise the position if:

- customers still describe Formalife simply as “a choking/first-aid course”;
- the distinction only exists in copy and not in delivery;
- most no-sales treat the offer as interchangeable with lower-priced alternatives;
- customers value a different attribute far more strongly than the proposed preparedness-system contrast;
- the broader Full confuses or weakens the choking-led entry;
- the operational cost of making the position true destroys contribution economics.
