# 1. Economic job

Funnel 01 must prove that Formalife can turn a real choking / complementary-feeding demand signal into an economically useful parent-first customer relationship.

Core chain:

**demand / trigger -> correct educational depth -> Guide or direct Full -> Full delivery -> proof/review -> referral -> customer relationship -> future real need.**

The funnel is not successful because it produces leads.

It is successful when Formalife can read:

- source/state;
- conversion;
- CAC;
- contribution;
- payback;
- delivery quality;
- review/proof production;
- referral economics;
- capacity.

Examples:

- brand/direct return;
- asks price/date;
- Guide buyer asks about Full;

## E-B — Trusted introduction

Examples:

- professional/healthcare introduction;
- trusted community introduction.

Route:

**trust transfer -> readiness router -> direct Full if ready OR correct education if not ready.**

## E-C — Problem/search discovery

Examples:

- fear of choking;
- starting solids questions;
- problem-aware organic/social/search;
- editorial/video discovery.

Route:

**problem education -> solution/category education -> Guide or Full based on readiness.**

## E-D — Latent / life-stage discovery

Examples:

- entering complementary feeding;
- content linked to developmental trigger without active course search.

Route:

**trigger relevance -> problem understanding -> solution/category -> Formalife/proof -> Guide/Full when ready.**

## E-E — Owned / returning relationship

Examples:

- existing lead;
- Guide buyer;
- previous no-sale;
- returning parent/new child;
- existing Formalife customer.

Route from **current state**, not from the top of Funnel 01.

# 6. First transaction paths

## Path A — Direct Full

For high-intent/product/brand-aware prospects:

**entry -> proof/offer -> 1 or 2 Caregivers -> confirmed or activation edition -> checkout.**

This is the shortest valid route.

## Path B — Guide first

Guide is an optional paid information/reference front-end at EUR 19.90.

It is not a mandatory toll booth.

Guide buyer may progress to Full using current Training Credit architecture:

- days 1–90: EUR 19.90 -> EUR 29.90 Full credit test;
- days 91–365: EUR 19.90 -> EUR 19.90 Full credit.

Existing Guide counts as included Guide on upgrade; do not create pointless duplicate fulfilment.

## Path C — Education first

For lower readiness:

**useful content -> permission/owned relationship where appropriate -> complete missing educational jobs -> Guide or Full.**

There is no universal nurture duration.

Accelerate when explicit intent appears; slow/pause when timing is the real barrier.

# 8. Risk reversal and activation

## Formalife Total Protection

Current direction:

- >7 days: refund or transfer;
- 7 days–48h: free transfer within 12 months or Formalife credit if no suitable date exists;
- <48h before start: one transfer for genuine family/child illness/material emergency communicated before start;
- no-show without notice: no cash refund; recovery route may be offered at 50% current price;
- Formalife cancellation: refund or priority transfer + EUR 20 future goodwill credit.

## Better-Than-Risk-Free Full guarantee

Attend the complete Full.

Within 24h, if customer believes the experience did not justify the amount paid:

- 100% refund of the Full amount paid;
- customer keeps Guide + Family Emergency Pack;
- unused Full service entitlements such as refresh end with refund.

No clinical-outcome guarantee is implied.

## Protected pre-enrolment

Selected editions may be:

**EDIZIONE IN ATTIVAZIONE — PRE-ISCRIZIONE PROTETTA.**

Preferred mechanism:

- pay EUR 19.90;
- receive Guide immediately;
- if edition activates -> payment becomes Training Credit;
- if it does not activate by published deadline -> refund EUR 19.90 and customer keeps Guide.

Current survival floor:

**5 paying participants**, because shareholder instructors currently create no instructor cash outflow.

This is a cash-survival floor, not normalized sustainable economics.

Normalized target remains **10–12 participants**.

# 9. Full delivery — canonical live arc

Canonical architecture: `FUNNEL_01_FULL_DELIVERY_SYSTEM_V5_CANONICAL.md`.

Integrated rehearsal clock: `FUNNEL_01_FULL_MASTER_RUN_OF_SHOW_V1.md`.

Current **250-minute / 4h10** architecture:

1. **ACT 0 — Open + experiential baseline — 12 min**
2. **ACT 1 — Il Problema — 6 min**
3. **ACT 2 — Ferma / Valuta / Agisci — 7 min**
4. **ACT 3 — Soffocamento — 72 min**
5. **Break — 10 min**
6. **ACT 4 — Rianimazione pediatrica / QCPR — 55 min**
7. **Mini reset — 5 min**
8. **ACT 5 — Altre emergenze — 48 min**
9. **ACT 6 — Scenari + complex Mirror Test — 25 min**
10. **ACT 7 — Continuity / household transfer / Pack / Pass / admin / final bookend — 10 min**

### Signature learning mechanic

Opening:

**consenting volunteer -> effective cough -> deterioration to ineffective cough -> no coaching -> capture starting behaviour.**

Ending:

**same volunteer if willing -> different, more complex scenario -> second caregiver/112 -> persistent obstruction -> loss of consciousness -> CPR transition -> AED.**

No humiliation; no staged perfect ending; correct errors openly.

### ACT 1 problem map

Founder-directed six-lens logic:

**domestic accidents -> choking observed/broader burden -> age distribution -> causes -> mortality/consequence -> protected meaning close.**

The course uses source-labelled observed data and estimates without blending them.

### ACT 2 Formalife mental protocol

Source: current choking Guide, Chapter 1.

**FERMA**
- respira;
- contatto con la realtà;
- verifica sicurezza.

**VALUTA**
- cosciente?;
- respira normalmente?;
- minaccia immediata visibile?

**AGISCI**
- chiama aiuto;
- manovra appropriata;
- monitora e adatta.

### Choking practice

Whole-room explanation/demo first.

Then:

**child station + infant station -> rotate -> guided practice -> correction -> repeat -> variable state -> no-prompt attempt -> reunite -> resolved vs unresponsive pathway.**

Current equipment decision:

- Act+Fast Red temporary child/adolescent workaround;
- one pediatric choking trainer to be acquired;
- 2 Little Baby QCPR support infant practice.

### CPR working sequence

Subject to final Mafalda/IRC sign-off:

**5 initial rescue breaths -> 30:2 -> AED as soon as available -> continue per 112/AED instructions.**

QCPR scored drill is compression-only:

**20 sec no coaching -> 20 sec one correction -> 20 sec full correction -> inspect graph.**

Ventilation is taught separately from the QCPR compression graph.

### Other emergencies

Teach one by one using:

**what it is -> prevention -> recognition -> what to do -> what not to do -> escalation/112/urgent evaluation.**

Current founder-approved pool:

- foreign-body ingestion — 8 min;
- head injury/fall — 6 min;
- febrile seizures — 5 min;
- poisoning — 4 min;
- burns — 5 min;
- anaphylaxis — 6 min;
- drowning — 7 min;
- severe bleeding/major trauma — 4 min;
- plus 2 min ACT 5 scope reset and **1 min protected buffer**.

`Severe breathing difficulty` is no longer a standalone ACT 5 topic.

### ACT 7 conclusion / continuity — founder-approved

ACT 6 remains the experiential peak. ACT 7 gives it meaning.

Sequence:

1. retrieve three critical anchors;
2. each participant writes one **24-hour household action**;
3. explain Guide / Pack / customer updates / free refresh by job;
4. explain two Pass Sicurezza + Guide Gift briefly;
5. complete certificates/admin **before** the final 2 min 15 sec;
6. return attention to the room and close the ACT 0/6 arc;
7. no group Q&A, sales restart or Google-review request after official close.

Current signature meaning:

**`Prepararsi non significa vivere aspettando che succeda un'emergenza. Significa poter vivere le giornate normali sapendo che, se qualcosa cambia, non dovrete inventare tutto da zero.`**

followed by:

**`Avete un modo per fermarvi, valutare, agire — e strumenti per continuare ad allenarlo. Questo era l'obiettivo di oggi.`**

Exact spoken copy may be polished later without changing the approved meaning/order.

# Final operating principle

Funnel 01 is intentionally large as a **system** and variable in length for the individual.

**low readiness -> more useful education and time**

**high readiness -> shorter path to purchase**

**purchase -> strong delivery -> proof/review -> referral -> useful relationship -> wait for the next real trigger.**

Do not force every person through every step.
