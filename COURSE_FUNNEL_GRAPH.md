# Formalife Course Funnel Graph

Status: PROVISIONAL STRATEGIC COURSE GRAPH — founder review pending
Date: 2026-09-21

Purpose: define a limited set of course-topic nodes that can cover the Formalife pediatric-safety territory without fragmenting the offer into dozens of SKUs, and define how customers can enter through different pains and be routed toward other relevant Formalife education.

This is a strategic topic graph, not a build backlog and not a final naming system. A course node can later be delivered online, in person, privately, through a partner, or in more than one format without becoming a different course merely because the delivery mode changes.

`FORMALIFE_BUILD_SEQUENCE.md` continues to govern implementation order. `FORMALIFE_COMMERCIAL_ARCHITECTURE.md` governs the wider acquisition/retention system.

## 1. Governing architecture

Formalife should not force every customer through one linear funnel.

The mature system should use multiple topic-specific entry points that converge into a common customer relationship and a small number of central capability-building products.

The current proposed core spine is:

**Pediatric Emergency Preparedness -> BLSD Formalife -> Skills Refresh / Rehearsal**

Around this spine sit life-stage and problem-specific entry nodes:

- New Baby Safety;
- Starting Solids & Feeding Safety;
- Choking & Disobstruction;
- Sickness & Red Flags;
- Home Safety & Babyproofing;
- Water Safety & Drowning Prevention;
- Car Seat & Road Safety;
- Travel / Outdoor / Seasonal Safety.

A separate certified BLSD lane serves credential demand, while Family & Caregiver Safety turns one trained customer into a safer care network and a natural referral/expansion loop.

No course should be deliberately incomplete only to force the next purchase. The next course must solve a distinct additional job, add depth, or address the next life-stage risk.

## 2. Proposed course universe — 13 nodes

### C01 — New Baby Safety

**Short description**

Safety-focused preparation for pregnancy through the first months: safe sleep, preparing the home, basic emergency readiness, medication/product safety, car-seat fundamentals, caregiver preparation and the safety decisions that matter immediately after bringing a baby home.

This is intentionally not a generic antenatal, breastfeeding or newborn-care course.

**Primary entry pain**

"The baby is arriving / has just arrived and I do not know what I need to make safe or what I should be prepared for."

**Useful courses before**

None. Designed as an autonomous early-life entry point.

**Natural next courses**

- C04 Pediatric Emergency Preparedness;
- C07 Sickness & Red Flags;
- C10 Car Seat & Road Safety for deeper competence;
- C08 Home Safety & Babyproofing when mobility approaches;
- C12 Family & Caregiver Safety when grandparents/babysitters enter the care network.

### C02 — Starting Solids & Feeding Safety

**Short description**

Complementary feeding / starting-solids education: readiness, food introduction, textures/cuts, feeding environment, allergen-related education and safe feeding habits. The preferred model is specialist-led/co-created rather than Formalife rebuilding an established feeding competence from zero.

**Primary entry pain**

"My baby is starting solids and I am confused about how to do it safely and correctly."

**Useful courses before**

None mandatory. C01 may be useful for families already inside Formalife.

**Natural next courses**

- C03 Choking & Disobstruction because feeding-safety knowledge does not replace emergency choking competence;
- C04 Pediatric Emergency Preparedness;
- C08 Home Safety & Babyproofing as the child becomes mobile.

### C03 — Choking & Disobstruction

**Short description**

Focused choking-prevention and response course: prevention, gagging/obstruction recognition where appropriate, what not to do, disobstruction maneuvers and supervised practice when delivered live. This node can support the current Light concept and a future digital version without becoming separate topics.

**Primary entry pain**

"I am afraid my child could choke and I would not know what to do."

**Useful courses before**

None. It must remain a valid direct entry point because choking/weaning is the strongest current Formalife demand wedge.

C02 is a complementary prerequisite only when the customer's problem is broader starting-solids education, not a mandatory prerequisite to learn disobstruction.

**Natural next courses**

- C04 Pediatric Emergency Preparedness — broader parent emergency capability;
- C05 BLSD Formalife — deeper resuscitation competence;
- C13 Skills Refresh / Rehearsal — later skill reinforcement;
- C12 Family & Caregiver Safety — extend preparedness to other caregivers.

### C04 — Pediatric Emergency Preparedness

**Short description**

Formalife's core parent-first flagship. Prepares parents/caregivers to recognize serious situations, act appropriately before professional help takes over, and practice the highest-value emergency skills. Current scope includes red flags/escalation, high-depth choking, meaningful CPR/AED introduction, severe respiratory/allergic emergencies, seizures and selected high-consequence injuries/exposures.

**Primary entry pain**

"I am responsible for a child, but if something serious happened I would not know how to recognize it or what to do first."

**Useful courses before**

None mandatory. High-intent customers must be able to enter Formalife directly here.

C01, C02, C03, C07, C08, C09, C10 or C11 may precede it when a specific life-stage problem created the relationship first.

**Natural next courses**

- C05 BLSD Formalife — deeper resuscitation/practice specialization;
- C07 Sickness & Red Flags — deeper non-procedural health-navigation knowledge;
- C08/C09/C10/C11 according to life stage and risk environment;
- C12 Family & Caregiver Safety;
- C13 Skills Refresh / Rehearsal.

### C05 — BLSD Formalife — non-certified proprietary course

**Short description**

Deep consumer resuscitation product optimized for learning rather than credential compliance: repeated deliberate practice, scenarios, CPR, AED, individual correction, refresh/reference and household-sharing logic. Non-certification is part of the product rationale rather than a hidden weakness.

**Primary entry pain**

"Knowing the basics is not enough for me; I want to be genuinely capable of performing resuscitation and using a DAE if I ever have to."

**Useful courses before**

C04 is the preferred predecessor because it creates broad emergency context, but direct entry remains possible for a sufficiently motivated customer.

C03 can also create the first awareness that emergency motor skills require practice.

**Natural next courses**

- C13 Skills Refresh / Rehearsal;
- C12 Family & Caregiver Safety / household skill transfer;
- C06 BLSD Certified only when the customer subsequently needs a formal credential.

### C06 — BLSD Certified

**Short description**

Standard certified BLSD path for customers who need the recognized credential. Curriculum and certification requirements remain those of the competent external system.

**Primary entry pain**

"I need a valid BLSD certification for work, volunteering, regulation or another formal requirement."

**Useful courses before**

None beyond any prerequisites imposed by the competent certification system.

**Natural next courses**

- C04 Pediatric Emergency Preparedness for the broader parent/caregiver safety context;
- C05 BLSD Formalife when the customer wants a learning/practice experience beyond the credential course;
- C13 Skills Refresh / Rehearsal when useful and compatible with certification renewal requirements.

This remains a separate credential-intent funnel rather than the center of the Formalife consumer brand.

### C07 — Sickness & Red Flags

**Short description**

Pediatrician-led parent education on recognizing a potentially sick child, common illness patterns, warning signs and the practical distinction between home observation, pediatric contact and urgent/emergency escalation. The course does not become a diagnostic or prescribing service.

**Primary entry pain**

"When my child is ill I do not know what is normal, what can wait and what should make me seek help immediately."

**Useful courses before**

None. Strong autonomous digital/reference entry point, especially for newborn/young-child families.

C01 can naturally precede it when acquired during pregnancy/newborn stage.

**Natural next courses**

- C04 Pediatric Emergency Preparedness — what to do when the problem moves from illness uncertainty to emergency response;
- C12 Family & Caregiver Safety for consistent escalation rules among caregivers;
- stage-specific safety courses as the child grows.

### C08 — Home Safety & Babyproofing

**Short description**

Systematic prevention of the major hazards created when a baby becomes mobile: falls, stairs/windows/furniture, burns/electricity, poisoning/medicines/chemicals, button batteries/magnets/small objects, bath safety and product/environment hazards.

**Primary entry pain**

"My child is starting to crawl/walk and suddenly the house feels full of risks I may not even see."

**Useful courses before**

None. It is a natural lifecycle entry course.

C01 is a lighter predecessor for families acquired before mobility.

**Natural next courses**

- C04 Pediatric Emergency Preparedness — prevention cannot eliminate every incident;
- C09 Water Safety & Drowning Prevention;
- C10 Car Seat & Road Safety;
- C11 Travel / Outdoor / Seasonal Safety;
- C12 Family & Caregiver Safety.

### C09 — Water Safety & Drowning Prevention

**Short description**

Prevention and preparedness around bath, pool, beach and open-water risk: supervision systems, environmental controls, age-appropriate protective measures, recognition of a water emergency and the bridge to resuscitation competence.

**Primary entry pain**

"My child is now around pools/sea/water and I know a moment of distraction can have serious consequences."

**Useful courses before**

None mandatory. Seasonal/life-event entry must remain possible.

C04 provides useful broad emergency context; C05 provides deeper resuscitation competence.

**Natural next courses**

- C05 BLSD Formalife — strongest depth link because drowning preparedness and resuscitation competence are closely connected;
- C11 Travel / Outdoor / Seasonal Safety;
- C13 Skills Refresh / Rehearsal before high-exposure seasons when appropriate.

### C10 — Car Seat & Road Safety

**Short description**

Child passenger and mobility safety: selecting and using age/size-appropriate restraint systems, common installation/use errors, transitions between seats, safe loading/unloading and age-appropriate road-safety fundamentals. Requires appropriate specialist competence before Formalife owns the product internally.

**Primary entry pain**

"I am carrying my child in the car but I am not sure the seat, setup or way I use it is actually safe."

**Useful courses before**

None. Strong purchase-event entry point around pregnancy, birth, seat transition or travel.

C01 can introduce the basics for new parents.

**Natural next courses**

- C11 Travel / Outdoor / Seasonal Safety;
- C04 Pediatric Emergency Preparedness;
- C08 Home Safety & Babyproofing for the broader prevention system.

### C11 — Travel, Outdoor & Seasonal Safety

**Short description**

Safety outside the normal home environment: holidays/hotels, travel preparation, beaches/pools, heat/sun/cold, playground/outdoor context, basic road/public-space risk and a family emergency-readiness checklist away from home. It complements rather than replaces deep car-seat and water-safety courses.

**Primary entry pain**

"We are travelling / spending more time outside the home and I want to know what changes in the risks and what I should prepare."

**Useful courses before**

None mandatory.

C09 and C10 are recommended when water exposure or car travel are central to the trip.

**Natural next courses**

- C04 Pediatric Emergency Preparedness if not yet completed;
- C09 Water Safety for families with recurring water exposure;
- C10 Car Seat & Road Safety for deeper mobility competence;
- C13 Skills Refresh when the trip exposes a stale practical skill.

### C12 — Family & Caregiver Safety

**Short description**

A compact family-system course on transferring safety rules and emergency readiness across parents, grandparents, babysitters and other caregivers: critical household rules, emergency contacts, medicines/allergies information, handover, who calls/does what, and when other caregivers need hands-on Formalife training themselves.

This is not a substitute for C03/C04/C05 practical training. Its commercial role is to convert one trained parent into a safer care network and make missing caregiver competence visible.

**Primary entry pain**

"I know what I would do, but my child is also cared for by other people and our rules/preparedness are inconsistent."

**Useful courses before**

Preferably C03 or C04 for the primary caregiver. It can also accompany C01 for a new baby entering an extended-family care network.

**Natural next courses**

- C03 for caregivers whose immediate gap is choking;
- C04 for caregivers who need broad emergency preparedness;
- C05 for caregivers who want deep resuscitation competence;
- C13 for previously trained caregivers whose skills need reinforcement.

### C13 — Skills Refresh & Rehearsal

**Short description**

Structured reinforcement of practical skills already learned: disobstruction, resuscitation, selected scenarios and updates. The content adapts to the customer's previous training and exists because practical confidence/skill should not be assumed permanent after one course.

**Primary entry pain**

"I took the course, but time has passed and I am no longer sure I would remember or perform the skills correctly."

**Useful courses before**

At least one practical course, principally C03, C04 or C05.

**Natural next courses**

- repeated refresh later when justified;
- C05 when the customer realizes they want deeper resuscitation capability;
- C12 when the household/care network needs alignment;
- any newly relevant lifecycle safety course.

## 3. The master funnel architecture

The 13 nodes should not produce 13 isolated funnels. The target is a few repeatable funnel patterns.

### Funnel A — Life-stage funnel

**pregnancy/newborn -> C01 New Baby Safety -> C04 Emergency Preparedness -> C07 Sickness & Red Flags -> C08 Home Safety as mobility begins -> C02 Starting Solids + C03 Choking when feeding transition begins -> C05 BLSD / C13 Refresh as depth/retention**

The exact order changes with the child's age. The point is to use predictable transitions to reactivate the same family rather than reacquire it from zero.

### Funnel B — Acute specific-pain funnel

**specific fear/problem -> specialist entry course -> C04 Emergency Preparedness -> relevant depth/adjacency -> C13 Refresh**

Examples:

- choking fear -> C03 -> C04 -> C05;
- water exposure -> C09 -> C05 and/or C04;
- home mobility risk -> C08 -> C04;
- car-seat uncertainty -> C10 -> C11 / C04;
- illness uncertainty -> C07 -> C04.

This is the main pattern for search, social and problem-specific lead generation.

### Funnel C — Emergency mastery funnel

**C03 or direct C04 -> C05 BLSD Formalife -> C12 Family & Caregiver Safety -> C13 Skills Refresh**

This is the deepest Formalife-owned capability path and can support the strongest high-value customer relationship inside emergency preparedness.

### Funnel D — Credential-intent funnel

**formal need -> C06 BLSD Certified -> C04 broader pediatric preparedness and/or C05 deeper consumer practice -> C13 refresh/renewal as appropriate**

Credential demand enters through a separate door but can be introduced to the wider Formalife system without pretending the certified course is the flagship brand product.

### Funnel E — Family-network expansion loop

**any strong Formalife experience -> C12 Family & Caregiver Safety -> identify untrained caregiver -> C03/C04/C05 for that caregiver -> household refresh / referral**

This is not merely a discount referral funnel. The commercial expansion is justified by a real safety gap: multiple people care for the same child.

## 4. Free layer and lead generation around the graph

Every major entry node can have its own free demand-capture layer without creating a separate business.

The governing pattern is:

**specific trigger/search/social content -> open useful resource or high-intent lead magnet -> nearest relevant course node -> common customer record -> cross-sell according to completed courses and life stage.**

Examples of asset roles include a home-safety audit, red-flags decision guide, safe-travel checklist, starting-solids resource, choking recognition resource, water-safety checklist or car-seat fit/selection tool once specialist capability exists.

High-intent prospects should be allowed to skip free assets and buy the relevant course directly.

## 5. Continuity layer above all courses

The course graph needs a continuity system, but not every retention mechanism must be a new course.

A future Formalife annual safety pass/membership can sit above the graph only when enough recurring value exists. Its job would be to keep the relationship active and route members toward the right next content/course rather than replace the course catalog.

Possible future continuity value:

- maintained digital reference library;
- safety updates;
- stage-based recommendations;
- recurring expert Q&A;
- refresh benefit/credits;
- caregiver/family access benefits;
- customer-only tools;
- preferential access to new modules/courses.

Membership is not currently approved as a build commitment. It is the natural future retention wrapper once the underlying content and recurring value are real.

## 6. Bundles are merchandising overlays, not new course nodes

Formalife can package existing course nodes into coherent bundles without creating new curricula every time.

Likely future bundle logics include:

- **New Baby Safety path** — C01 + C04 + selected C10 content/depth;
- **Starting Solids Safety path** — C02 + C03;
- **Emergency Mastery path** — C04 + C05 + C13;
- **Safe Home / Mobile Child path** — C08 + C04 + relevant C12;
- **Water / Summer / Travel path** — C09 + C11 + relevant C10;
- **Family Preparedness path** — C04 + C12 + caregiver seats / refresh.

Bundle composition and pricing remain separate offer-design decisions and should be justified by economics rather than by catalog aesthetics.

## 7. Strategic exclusions

Formalife should not add generic parenting courses merely because they share the same parent audience.

Current exclusions from the Formalife-owned course graph include:

- full generic antenatal/preparto education;
- breastfeeding as a general discipline;
- generic infant sleep/sleep coaching;
- crying/soothing as a general parenting discipline;
- motor development;
- language development;
- tantrums/rules/emotional education;
- generic pedagogy/development.

Narrow safety slices remain admissible: safe sleep fits C01; a general sleep course does not.

Adjacent excluded domains may later be served through specialist partnership/cross-promotion or a separate brand architecture if the economics justify it.

## 8. Commercial logic

This graph solves the current short-funnel problem by giving Formalife three types of monetization after any entry point:

1. **core conversion** — move a specific-problem customer toward C04 where broader preparedness is genuinely relevant;
2. **depth** — C05/C13 and other specialist practice increase competence on an existing safety job;
3. **horizontal/lifecycle expansion** — new stages expose new safety problems and route the existing customer to C01/C02/C07/C08/C09/C10/C11/C12 as appropriate.

The customer therefore does not need to follow one universal sequence. The commercial system must know what the customer already completed, what life-stage problem is current and what the next relevant gap is.

This preserves the Layer 1 principle that the second purchase should be designed while avoiding the unethical/weak pattern of deliberately making the first purchase incomplete.

## 9. Current strategic status

This 13-node graph is a **PROPOSAL**, not yet a founder-approved final curriculum universe.

If approved, it should supersede the looser product-topic lists in `OFFER_UNIVERSE_MAP.md` as the canonical course-level graph, while that file remains useful for the broader thematic universe.

Implementation order remains governed by `FORMALIFE_BUILD_SEQUENCE.md`; approval of the graph would not authorize simultaneous production of all 13 courses.
