# 1. Course Rail override — ACT 5

V0.1 stated that the Course Rail normally appears on ACT 5 topic slides.

Final-audit decision supersedes that rule for S45–S56.

## S45–S56

Hide/suppress the seven-segment Course Rail.

May retain only a small chapter label:

**`ALTRE EMERGENZE`**

The dominant persistent navigation becomes the ACT 5 grammar:

**COS'È | PREVIENI | RICONOSCI | FAI | NON FARE | ESCALA**

Reason:

two persistent navigation systems at comparable visual weight create unnecessary competition. The six-step local grammar has the stronger immediate learning job inside ACT 5.

The Course Rail remains available before/after ACT 5 under the V0.1 rules.

# 2. S65 continuity-system design lock

S65 must display four functions with equal structural weight:

- GUIDE;
- PACK;
- AGGIORNAMENTI;
- REFRESH.

Do not use:

- crossed-out prices;
- price anchoring;
- value badges;
- `bonus` labels;
- gift-stack/e-commerce composition;
- invented standalone values.

Preferred grammar:

stable 2x2 system map / four functional nodes with restrained progressive reveal.

The slide is a continuity-system explanation, not an offer-stack slide.

---

# 3. S67 Pass Sicurezza treatment

The referral mechanic does not receive a projected promotional layout.

During S67:

- projected surface remains operational/access-oriented;
- physical Pass Sicurezza is the visual object for the referral explanation;
- avoid oversized referral headline, reward badge, arrows to conversion funnel or promotional CTA treatment.

The visual hierarchy must keep the room in `handoff / access / continuity`, not restart acquisition psychology before the final close.

---

# 4. S62 debrief treatment

S62 is question-first and outcome-neutral.

Headline:

**`CHE COSA È CAMBIATO RISPETTO ALL'INIZIO?`**

Do not pre-populate positive achievement claims.

Before participant/facilitator evidence is spoken, keep the right-side/current-state area visually neutral or empty.

Only observed labels are revealed.

Do not use score gauges, before/after percentage improvement, badges or success colors as a default outcome claim.

---

# 5. Appendix treatment for S07 / S33

S07 and S33 move to appendix/reference.

Appendix slides may be denser than the main learning deck but must still:

- preserve source / period / population;
- make evidence-status boundaries explicit;
- avoid visual comparison between non-comparable denominators;
- clearly label adult evidence as adult where relevant.

They do not appear in normal slide progression and do not carry the main Course Rail requirement.
