# 1. Operating contract

Current working event length:

**250 minutes / 4h10 from official start to official close.**

This is a rehearsal baseline, not a claim that every first edition will hit the clock exactly.

Rehearsal rule:

**measure first; revise from evidence.**

Do not protect weak explanation at the expense of corrected practice, retrieval or the protected final close.

Priority when the course runs late:

1. remove explanation redundancy;
2. shorten low-value transitions;
3. use ACT 5 protected buffer;
4. simplify non-core examples;
5. only then reconsider scope in a later revision.

Do **not** silently recover time by:

- cutting choking corrected practice;
- cutting the QCPR drill;
- eliminating the real break;
- rushing the Complex Mirror Test debrief;
- turning ACT 7 into a certificate queue;
- deleting the final official bookend.

## 00:18–00:25 — ACT 2 — FERMA / VALUTA / AGISCI

Owner: Instructor A.

Source of truth: current choking Guide, Chapter 1.

### 00:18–00:18:40 — Why a mental protocol

Emergency stress can drive automatic response; the course needs one repeatable orientation/action structure.

### 00:18:40–00:20:35 — FERMA

Teach exactly:

1. respira;
2. stabilisci il contatto con la realtà — `Dove sei? Chi c'è con te? Cosa vedi?`;
3. verifica che tu stesso sia al sicuro.

Book timing concept: **2–3 conscious seconds**, not a prolonged pause.

### 00:20:35–00:22:15 — VALUTA

Three questions:

1. è cosciente?;
2. respira normalmente?;
3. c'è una minaccia immediata visibile?

### 00:22:15–00:24:20 — AGISCI

Universal sequence:

1. chiama aiuto;
2. manovra appropriata;
3. monitora e adatta.

Technical 112 timing remains scenario-specific later.

### 00:24:20–00:25:00 — Retrieval + bridge

Whole room reconstructs F/V/A.

Then:

**now build the technical action for choking.**
