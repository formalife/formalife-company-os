# 1. Deck contract

This is a **live-event deck**, not a projected handbook.

Rules:

1. **One main job per slide/state.** A slide may contain several related data points only when they answer the same question.
2. **Slides do not replace demonstration or practice.** During motor-skill teaching, the screen either supports the single relevant decision/landmark or becomes a neutral hold state.
3. **No dense reference-document slides.** Detail belongs in the Guide, Pack, digital reference layer or instructor notes.
4. **Clinical wording stays short.** The instructor explains; the slide anchors.
5. **Every statistic shows source + year/period.** Denominator/geography must remain visible where meaning changes.
6. **Observed data and estimates must look different.** Do not make Ministry estimates, hospital records and mortality shares visually equivalent.
7. **The same visual grammar repeats where useful.** ACT 5 especially must feel like one system rather than eight unrelated mini-decks.
8. **Practice screens are operational tools.** They may show sequence, roles, rotation or the learning loop; they must not invite participants to stare at the screen instead of the trainer/manikin.
9. **No public scoring screens.** QCPR comparison is learner-vs-own-baseline, not leaderboard.
10. **ACT 0 and ACT 6 protect the simulation.** Do not reveal upcoming state changes on the screen.
11. **The official final slide is not commercial.** No QR, referral, certificate or product-stack visual after the administrative handoff.
12. The first rehearsal may collapse or split slides. **The map is complete; the physical page count is still a test variable.**

Working count:

- **73 active slide states** across the official 4h10 course;
- **7 hidden ACT 6 scenario-bank slides**, of which only 3 are activated per edition;
- **1 optional pre-start holding screen** outside the official clock.

Some active states may later be implemented as builds/animations of the same physical slide, so the final PowerPoint/Canva page count may be lower.

# 13. Visual production families

To avoid designing 73 unrelated pages, later deck production should use a small reusable system.

## Family A — Narrative / section meaning

Used for:
- A0-01;
- A1-06;
- A3-16;
- A6-08;
- A7-06/A7-07.

Characteristics:
- large visual or single line;
- minimal text;
- high whitespace;
- used for transition/meaning, not information density.

## Family B — Data visual

Used for ACT 1 and adult/pediatric survival evidence.

Characteristics:
- one question/headline;
- one chart or number hierarchy;
- source/year footer always visible;
- observed vs estimated encoded differently.

## Family C — Decision fork / algorithm

Used for choking, CPR, 112, ACT 5 escalation.

Characteristics:
- maximum 2–4 branches/actions;
- clear directional flow;
- age/state visually obvious;
- no paragraph text.

## Family D — Anatomy / technique landmark

Used for airway, child/infant demo deconstruction, QCPR basics.

Characteristics:
- one clean illustration/manikin image;
- arrows/landmarks only where action changes;
- labels large enough for room viewing.

## Family E — Practice hold / operational

Used for station lab, QCPR lab, break, material handoff.

Characteristics:
- roles / sequence / timer / rotation only;
- readable at distance;
- designed to reduce instructor repetition.

## Family F — ACT 5 six-step grammar

Characteristics:
- persistent strip: `COS'È / PREVIENI / RICONOSCI / FAI / NON FARE / ESCALA`;
- one dominant topic-specific visual;
- one anchor line;
- same grid every time.

## Family G — Scenario

Characteristics:
- contextual image + sparse facts;
- no answer cues before response;
- correction/anchor appears only after the learner commits.

# 14. Copy-density limits for later slide production

Working production rules:

- narrative/transition slide: **≤12 words preferred**;
- data slide: headline + chart + source; no explanatory paragraph;
- decision/algorithm slide: **≤6 action nodes**;
- ACT 5 main slide: no more than **1–2 bullets per active grammar section** visible at once; use build/reveal rather than projecting the whole reference sheet;
- demo landmark slide: **≤5 labelled landmarks**;
- practice hold slide: **≤5 steps**;
- scenario slide: **≤4 facts/cues** before learner response;
- final close: **one line or no text**.

These are design constraints, not scientific laws; rehearsal and room legibility decide final implementation.

# 15. Content that should NOT become slides

Do not create dedicated slides for:

- long instructor biographies;
- company history;
- every clinical caveat that belongs in notes/reference;
- every prohibited action if one grouped visual suffices;
- detailed legal terms of Total Protection/BTRF;
- every Pack component;
- review request at T=0;
- testimonial carousel inside the course;
- full bibliography pages during live teaching;
- long Q&A parking lot;
- certificate wording;
- referral economics / Guide Gift internal cost;
- rehearsal metrics;
- equipment inventory.

Sources/bibliography can exist in hidden appendix/reference slides if operationally useful, but they are not part of the active 73-state learning sequence.

# 17. Rehearsal questions specific to the slide map

During the first full rehearsal, record for each slide/state:

- Did the slide reduce explanation time or add explanation time?
- Did participants look at the correct thing — screen vs instructor/manikin?
- Was the slide readable from the back of the room?
- Did any slide reveal the answer before a retrieval/scenario response?
- Did any data slide require too much caveat to be useful live?
- Did any ACT 5 slide become a projected checklist rather than an anchor?
- Did demo slides compete with the live demonstration?
- Did practice hold screens reduce repeated instructions?
- Did the final commercial/operational slide disappear before the final bookend?
- Which adjacent slides can be collapsed without losing a learning job?
- Which single slide carries too many jobs and needs splitting?

Revision labels after rehearsal:

- `KEEP`;
- `COLLAPSE`;
- `SPLIT`;
- `REWRITE`;
- `VISUAL REPLACE`;
- `MOVE TO NOTES`;
- `MOVE TO PACK/REFERENCE`;
- `HIDE / APPENDIX`.
