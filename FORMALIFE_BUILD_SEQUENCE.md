# Formalife Build Sequence

Status: CURRENT — founder-authorized strategic construction sequence
Date: 2026-09-21

Purpose: translate `FORMALIFE_TARGET_MODEL.md` into the order in which Formalife should actually be built. This is not a feature backlog. The sequence is causal: each block must make the next one more valuable and less risky.

## Governing principle

Formalife should not attempt to build the full target company at once.

The near-term job is to create one complete commercial engine around the strongest existing wedge, make it economically legible and repeatable, then add scalable products and adjacent verticals.

The sequencing rule is:

**focused flagship -> repeatable trusted distribution -> scalable digital monetization -> owned demand/lifecycle system -> adjacent expansion.**

This deliberately increases the sophistication of the overall system while limiting the number of variables being introduced at the same time.

## Block 1 — Make the choking-led flagship system the commercial proof engine

The first priority is not a new product. It is to make the current **choking-led acquisition system** strong enough to feed a coherent broader pediatric-safety/emergency-preparedness flagship and function as the first commercial proof engine of the target company.

The commercial system is:

**choking-specific Guide / appropriate low-friction Light or direct entry -> broader Full Course -> designed next step.**

Choking/weaning remains the primary acquisition wedge; the rebuilt Full Course is the broader parent-first pediatric safety/emergency-preparedness flagship. The system must not collapse those two jobs into the same market meaning.

The flagship must present Formalife as a parent-first pediatric-safety company, not as another generic training provider.

The minimum system includes:

- visible human/scientific authority;
- clear customer-facing distinction between Formalife consumer education and certification-led training;
- the Guide/Light choking entry assets and broader Full Course working as one coherent commercial system without making the entry product deliberately incomplete;
- simple Single/Couple architecture;
- reliable checkout, attribution and customer data capture;
- real proof from previous/current customers;
- explicit capture of source, relevant child life stage/trigger where voluntarily provided and useful, and purchased product;
- normalized delivery economics including instructor cost.

The immediate economic proof remains the current founder target of 10-12 paying participants at EUR 80 Single / EUR 120 Couple, but the measurement unit expands from one edition to the full customer relationship.

### Gate before expansion

Formalife must be able to read reliably:

- revenue and normalized contribution per edition;
- source of each customer;
- conversion by source/partner when observable;
- customer count and purchase history;
- second-purchase / upgrade behavior when offered;
- whether the choking-specific entry -> broader Full Course bridge is actually understood and accepted by customers.

The goal of Block 1 is not perfect branding. It is a flagship system that can generate money, proof and usable data without founder-side improvisation.

## Block 2 — Turn the pediatrician success into a repeatable distribution system

Before relying on paid cold acquisition, Formalife should attempt to reproduce the strongest existing commercial evidence: trusted distribution through people and organizations that already serve the right parents.

The current pediatrician relationship is treated as the prototype, not as a permanent single-source channel.

The distribution system should be productized for repeatable use with:

- pediatricians and pediatric/perinatal professionals;
- maternity/family structures;
- nurseries/preschools and parent communities;
- selected family-health/community partners.

Formalife retains a consistent central offer, customer journey, payment/data system and delivery standard while partners provide trust and access.

### Gate before treating the channel as scalable

The company must be able to distinguish:

- partner that merely expressed interest;
- partner that actively promoted/hosted;
- participants/customers generated;
- economics of the relationship;
- repeatability over more than one initiative.

The strategic objective is to move from **one pediatrician relationship** to a **partner-distribution capability**.

## Block 3 — Build one scalable digital flagship, not a digital catalog

Only after the flagship and customer path are coherent should Formalife build the first substantial digital product.

The first digital product should stay very close to the proven core: pediatric choking / emergency preparation for parents and caregivers.

It should be a real standalone consumer product, not a recorded webinar and not a deliberately crippled teaser.

Preferred characteristics include:

- high production quality;
- clear expert/scientific provenance;
- long-lived or lifetime access where appropriate;
- integration with the Guide/reference system;
- structured bridge to live practical education where hands-on practice creates additional value;
- ability to sell nationally without proportional classroom capacity.

The job of this product is to create the first meaningful scalable revenue layer and to prove that Formalife can monetize outside physical classroom capacity.

### Gate before broadening the digital library

Measure at least:

- conversion into the digital product;
- gross contribution;
- completion/usage signals available;
- upgrades into live products or other purchases;
- customer support burden;
- whether the product can be sold without founder-intensive explanation.

Do not build a large library merely to resemble Tiny Hearts or Genitori.app. One successful scalable product is worth more than ten unfinished modules.

## Block 4 — Build owned demand and lifecycle infrastructure around products that already monetize

Once Formalife has a coherent live flagship and at least one scalable digital monetization path, it should build a stronger owned-demand engine.

This includes two connected capabilities.

### Recurring free utility/value

Formalife should create a useful safety resource/tool that parents return to because it solves a recurring problem, not because it is a disguised advertisement.

The exact utility is still an open research problem and should not be invented casually. Its role is to create repeated use, search/social discovery, trust and first-party audience growth that naturally connects to Formalife paid products.

### Lifecycle CRM/account logic

Formalife should know enough, lawfully and usefully, to avoid treating every customer as anonymous.

The mature lifecycle architecture is trigger-based, not a rigid age calendar. The working model is defined in `LIFECYCLE_STATE_TIMELINE.md` and `LIFECYCLE_FUNNEL_PATHS.md`.

Relevant future dimensions include:

- products purchased/completed;
- source/partner;
- caregiver role where useful;
- broad child/family life stage where voluntarily provided;
- the real trigger/need that brought the customer in;
- next plausible safety need only when a real transition or exposure makes it relevant.

The objective is to turn acquisition into a customer relationship and design the second purchase rather than waiting for it accidentally, while preserving a legitimate `WAITING FOR TRIGGER` state when no next paid need is active.

## Block 5 — Expand through the closest high-leverage adjacency

Only after the core engine works should Formalife materially expand the offer set.

The first preferred expansion path is **complementary specialist partnership** rather than internal duplication.

### Weaning partnership

Svezzy is the current strongest candidate because weaning is already highly adjacent to Formalife's choking entry window while Svezzy owns specialist assets Formalife should not rebuild from zero.

Potential joint products include:

- co-created publishing;
- digital education;
- bundles or other integrated offers.

The partnership should preserve real specialist identity and create value for both companies, with explicit economics, IP and customer/data rules.

### Proprietary BLSD track

The two-track BLSD architecture remains part of the target model but should not distract from proving the initial parent-safety engine.

When developed:

- certified BLSD serves the credential job;
- non-certified BLSD Formalife becomes a proprietary learning product optimized for preparedness rather than certification, with freedom to design practice, scenarios, repetition, refresh and household sharing.

The non-certified product can become a second strong Formalife flagship once the company has already learned how to launch, distribute and monetize a proprietary consumer training product.

## What remains deliberately later

The following belong to the target company but are not first-order construction priorities:

- broad multi-topic digital library;
- large physical-product catalog;
- national instructor/franchise/licensing network;
- sponsor/third-party-funded mass access;
- broad geographic scale;
- complex membership/subscription architecture;
- generic parenting categories outside pediatric safety/prevention/emergency preparedness.

They become rational only when the earlier engine creates demand, economics, proof and operating standards worth scaling.

## Current executive sequence

1. **Flagship commercial proof** — make the choking-led Guide/Light -> broader Full Course path work as one measurable parent-first engine.
2. **Trusted distribution** — reproduce the pediatrician mechanism across multiple relevant partners.
3. **Scalable digital flagship** — create the first nationally sellable, high-quality consumer digital product around the same proven core.
4. **Owned demand + lifecycle** — recurring free utility and trigger-based customer relationship infrastructure tied to working paid products.
5. **Adjacent expansion** — specialist partnership first; proprietary BLSD and broader vertical depth after the core proves repeatable.
