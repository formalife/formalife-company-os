## Purpose

Validate whether funded Centri per la Famiglia can become a repeatable institutional B2B/B2B2C channel for Formalife through a dedicated **Programma Sicurezza Pediatrica per le Famiglie**.

This file records a test architecture, not a proven offer, price list or scalable channel.

## HYPOTHESIS — institutional offer

Working name: **Programma Sicurezza Pediatrica per le Famiglie**.

The hypothesis is a modular institutional program rather than a standalone course or standalone book. Depending on buyer need and procurement fit, the program may combine:

1. practical educational sessions for parents/caregivers;
2. durable reference material, including the Guide when appropriate;
3. pre/post educational materials and participant communication support;
4. support to activation/registration where useful;
5. attendance and feedback collection;
6. concise activity reporting for the commissioning Center;
7. multi-edition / multi-site deployment across Hub, Spoke or municipalities when required.

Exact clinical/educational scope remains subject to scientific validation before customer-facing use.

The program is consumer/family education and must not be represented as professional certification or medical advice.

## Outreach rule

The first email has one job: obtain a relevant 15–20 minute qualification conversation or routing to the correct decision/process owner.

It should:

- demonstrate knowledge of the specific Center/project without leading with the amount funded;
- explain the institutional-program hypothesis briefly;
- avoid sending a fixed quote before qualification;
- avoid inventing budget availability or procurement eligibility;
- ask whether the program could fit activities already planned and who should evaluate it.

Founder operational note: outreach is sent manually from Formalife's professional Aruba email. Outcomes are reported manually back into the operating process; do not assume Gmail captures these sends.

## Qualification — minimum evidence required

Before a commercial proposal, understand at least:

1. **Current program / problem** — what 0–6, first-1000-days, prevention or pediatric-safety activities are already planned or delivered? What gap remains?
2. **Budget / eligibility** — is relevant budget still programmable? Is external pediatric-safety/family education admissible and rendicontabile? How are teaching, materials/books and logistics treated?
3. **Scope** — how many families, editions, sites or municipalities would make the activity useful?
4. **Decision** — who sponsors, approves, validates and can block the purchase?
5. **Procurement** — what supplier/onboarding/quotation/affidamento process applies?
6. **Timing** — by when must the activity be approved, contracted and delivered?
7. **Future-decision test** — if Formalife supplied a proposal matching the agreed scope and investment constraints, what would happen next internally?

## Proposal gate

Do not send a customized commercial proposal merely because a contact replies.

A proposal becomes justified when there is sufficient clarity on:

- real program fit;
- buyer/beneficiary configuration;
- budget/eligibility or at least credible purchasing path;
- scope;
- decision process;
- procurement;
- timing.

If these remain materially opaque, the next step is qualification, stakeholder involvement, recontact at the right time or disqualification — not a speculative preventivo.

## Test review gate

After the six first-wave prospects have gone through initial outreach plus a reasonable follow-up cycle, review before expanding to P2s or the rest of Lombardia.

Review evidence:

- responses and routing quality;
- qualified conversations obtained;
- confirmed program/eligibility fit;
- common objections or existing alternatives;
- procurement patterns;
- scope requested;
- proposal requests / concrete next commercial steps;
- evidence that Formalife's program is materially different from isolated free/low-cost first-aid events;
- economics once real requested configurations exist.

If the first wave produces no qualified opportunities, diagnose fit, timing, procurement and offer before increasing outreach volume.

## Pricing decision rule

Pricing remains an **OPEN QUESTION** during first-wave discovery.

Do not infer institutional pricing from current B2C Full pricing, historical B2B designs or grant amount.

After sufficient qualification conversations, design pricing from:

- requested scope;
- normalized delivery cost;
- travel/logistics;
- materials / Guide quantities;
- administrative/reporting burden;
- capacity;
- procurement realities;
- contribution and cash economics.
