# 2. Live duration — benchmark and decision

Representative current parent/caregiver offers checked 2026-09-22:

- Tiny Hearts public Baby & Child First Aid: 4 hours;
- British Red Cross First Aid for Baby and Child: 4 hours;
- CPR Kids Baby + Child CPR & First Aid: approximately 3h15;
- Safe Beginnings essentials: approximately 2h–2h15; broader certification-style course approximately 5 hours;
- Mini First Aid: generally 2 hours;
- Daisy First Aid: approximately 2 hours;
- Camillo Rescue Baby 2.0: approximately 3 hours;
- Italian CRI examples: commonly around 3 hours for lifesaving/choking/CPR, with some 4-hour formats.

Interpretation:

- 2 hours fits essentials/narrow classes;
- 3 hours fits choking + CPR + selected first aid;
- 4 hours is a validated consumer format for broader parent-first training;
- 5 hours begins to impose materially more commitment/fatigue and often accompanies broader/certification-style training.

FOUNDER-APPROVED DIRECTION:

- approximately 3h50–4h00 meaningful teaching/practice;
- approximately 10–20 minutes break/transition;
- calendar block approximately 4h10–4h20;
- lower-priority theory moves to pre/post layers rather than extending the live event to five hours.

# 3. Formalife Total Protection — founder-approved direction

Competitive research showed that many parent-course operators protect the provider more strongly than the customer, often limiting refunds/transfers.

Formalife will deliberately use stronger customer protection, subject to final legal wording.

## Confirmed edition

- >7 days before: full refund OR free transfer;
- 7 days to 48h before: one free transfer within 12 months OR 100% Formalife credit if no suitable date exists yet;
- <48h before, where genuine family/child illness or material emergency is communicated before start: one free transfer;
- initial V1 does not require a medical certificate unless abuse becomes material;
- no-show without notice: no cash refund; test one recovery booking at 50% of then-current public price, subject to capacity.

## Formalife cancellation

If Formalife cancels a confirmed edition:

- customer chooses full refund or priority transfer;
- customer also receives EUR 20 future Formalife goodwill credit, with expiry/use rules to be standardized.

Exact legal wording remains OPEN.

# 4. Pre-enrolment / activation editions — founder-approved mechanism

Some editions may be launched as:

**EDIZIONE IN ATTIVAZIONE — PRE-ISCRIZIONE PROTETTA**

The activation condition must be conspicuous before commitment.

Customer sees:

- edition is not yet confirmed;
- published minimum threshold;
- published confirmation deadline;
- payment/refund mechanics;
- what happens if the course activates or fails to activate.

## Preferred model — Guide-backed Better-Than-Risk-Free pre-enrolment

Customer pays EUR 19.90 and immediately receives the Guide.

If edition activates:

- Guide payment becomes Training Credit;
- Momentum Credit can apply when within the approved time window;
- customer pays the Full balance.

If edition does not activate by the deadline:

- Formalife automatically refunds EUR 19.90;
- customer keeps the Guide.

Current failed-activation cash exposure per pre-enrollee is approximately EUR 14.15 before CAC/overhead, from Guide printing + packaging + shipping + lost Stripe fee.

Use selectively rather than making every edition contingent.

## Minimum threshold

The previous provisional 8-person general floor is superseded after receiving fuller cost data.

Current planning recommendation:

- Brescia / low- or zero-CAC activation test: **10 paying participants** as provisional public floor unless the booked mix justifies another threshold;
- cold paid acquisition: do not rely on headcount alone until CAC is known;
- publish the threshold per edition before pre-enrolment.

Reason: 2-Caregiver orders reduce contribution per occupied seat, so headcount alone is an incomplete economic measure.

# 5. Training Credit — founder-approved test

## 100% Standard Credit

Days 91–365 after Guide purchase:

**EUR 19.90 -> EUR 19.90 Full credit.**

## 150% Momentum Credit

First 90 days:

**EUR 19.90 -> EUR 29.90 Full credit.**

## Duplicate Guide rule

The Guide already purchased counts as the participant's included Full Guide.

Do not automatically issue a duplicate copy to the same participant.

For 2 Caregivers, provide a second Guide when the second participant does not already own one.

## Household credit

Two separate Guide buyers may combine credit toward 2 Caregivers. Momentum path starts with a controlled total cap of approximately EUR 40–50 until real economics are observed.

## Revised economics

With current founder cost inputs:

- direct 1-Caregiver Full pre-fixed/pre-CAC contribution: ~EUR 54.42;
- Guide -> Full with 100% credit: ~EUR 49.12 across the relationship;
- Guide -> Full with 150% Momentum: ~EUR 41.07 across the relationship.

Thus 150% Momentum has a real contribution cost and must earn its place through better acquisition/conversion/time-to-Full economics.

**Attend the complete Full Course. If within 24 hours you believe the experience did not justify what you paid, Formalife refunds 100% of the Full amount actually paid. The Guida Anti-Panico and Family Emergency Pack remain with the customer for the time and trust invested.**
