## 8.2 FOUNDER DECISION — customer protection / risk reversal

### Formalife Total Protection

Direction approved:

- >7 days: full refund or transfer;
- 7 days–48h: one free transfer within 12 months or 100% Formalife credit if no suitable date exists;
- <48h: one free transfer for genuine family/child illness/material emergency communicated before start;
- no-show without notice: no refund; test one recovery booking at 50% current price;
- if Formalife cancels confirmed edition: refund or priority transfer + EUR 20 future goodwill credit.

Final legal wording remains to be reviewed.

### Better-Than-Risk-Free Full guarantee

Approved direction:

- attend full course;
- within 24h, if customer believes experience did not justify amount paid, Formalife refunds 100% of Full amount actually paid;
- customer keeps Guide + Family Emergency Pack;
- refund terminates unused future Full service entitlements such as free retraining;
- no guarantee of clinical/emergency outcome is implied.
