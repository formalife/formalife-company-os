# 1. Founder decision / phase boundary

PowerPoint construction is considered complete as an active workstream.

Rehearsal, final clinical/scientific QA and projector verification remain pre-delivery gates but are intentionally deferred while the rest of Funnel 01 is operationalized.

The active workstream is now:

**FULL OFFER OPERATIONALIZATION.**

---

# 2. Outcome of this phase

Phase is complete when Formalife can take a real Full customer from purchase to post-course relationship with defined rules, assets, ownership and measurement.

Minimum end-to-end chain:

**purchase / pre-enrolment -> confirmation -> fit/logistics -> pre-course orientation -> attendance -> Guide/Pack/certificate -> service/guarantee handling -> feedback/proof -> referral -> customer stream -> refresh entitlement -> lifecycle waiting/next trigger.**

The company must know what happens at every material exception:

- activating edition;
- confirmed edition;
- second-caregiver addition;
- transfer/cancellation;
- no-show;
- Formalife cancellation;
- BTRF request;
- service issue;
- refresh booking/no-show;
- referral reward fulfilment.

---

# 3. Causal build order

## O1 — Full fulfilment operating contract — FIRST

Define the end-to-end service contract from purchase through customer relationship.

Required outputs:

- stage/trigger map;
- owner per stage;
- customer communication job;
- data/event created;
- exception routing;
- suppression logic;
- proof capture point;
- unresolved parameters clearly marked OPEN.

Canonical child:

`FUNNEL_01_FULL_FULFILMENT_OPERATING_CONTRACT_V1.md`.

## O2 — BEFORE system

Build only what the live experience genuinely needs before attendance:

1. purchase/fit/logistics confirmation;
2. concise pre-course orientation;
3. reminder sequence;
4. participant / second-caregiver operational information;
5. support route.

Do not build a second digital course.

## O3 — Family Emergency Pack production spec

Turn the current approved pack direction into a manufacturable V1.

Required:

- exact component list;
- copy/content job per component;
- physical size/material/finish;
- quantity per household/order;
- duplicate pieces for second caregiver where useful;
- versioning/date control;
- QR/reference-hub dependencies;
- supplier quote/BOM;
- assembly time;
- replacement/reprint rule.

Current EUR 5/pack remains a planning estimate until supplier evidence replaces it.

## O4 — Refresh operating system

Operationalize one free practical refresh within 12 months.

Must define:

- eligibility;
- booking window;
- capacity reservation rule;
- paid-seat protection;
- reminder;
- cancellation/no-show;
- redemption event;
- expiry;
- exception handling;
- metric set.

Do not treat current zero cash cost as zero capacity cost.

## O5 — Customer-protection fulfilment procedures

Translate approved policy architecture into procedures for:

- >7d refund/transfer;
- 7d–48h transfer/credit;
- <48h family emergency transfer;
- no-show recovery route;
- Formalife cancellation + EUR 20 goodwill credit;
- protected activation edition;
- Better-Than-Risk-Free refund.

Customer-facing legal wording can be finalized later, but operational actions, owner, records and accounting consequences must be defined before launch.

## O6 — AFTER / customer relationship stream

Define the minimum operating rhythm that keeps the customer relationship useful without inventing an artificial subscription.

Must distinguish:

- transactional/service communication;
- editorial/marketing permission;
- refresh communication;
- referral communication;
- new-trigger/lifecycle handoff.

Initial cadence is a test, not doctrine.

## O7 — Proof production system

Every rebuilt edition is an evidence-production event.

Operationalize capture at:

- before purchase / registration where low friction;
- during delivery;
- immediately after;
- T+24h;
- later usage/refresh/referral points.

Preserve provenance and permission.

Do not convert satisfaction into clinical-outcome evidence.

## O8 — Checkout / attribution / CRM minimum

Before interpreting commercial results, implement the minimum G5 instrumentation needed for the first active-demand routes:

- one usable customer identity;
- purchase/product/date history;
- 1 vs 2 Caregivers;
- source/referrer/campaign where available;
- Full checkout start / purchase / attendance / no-show;
- barrier/waitlist where captured;
- consent state;
- service issue state;
- referral relationship;
- refresh entitlement/redemption;
- revenue/contribution fields sufficient for route economics.

No unnecessary CRM fields.

## O9 — First active-demand route activation

Only after enough of O1–O8 is operational to make a real sale fulfilable and measurable, activate the shortest commercial route defined by Funnel 01 Phase 1:

**E-A active intent + incoming E-B trusted inbound -> J3/J4 as needed -> Full offer/date/checkout -> onboarding -> delivery -> proof/referral.**

Do not build cold/latent acquisition first.

4. refresh capacity reservation/no-show rules;

6. exact customer-stream cadence;
7. final legal/customer wording of policies;
8. final clinical/scientific claims before delivery;
9. CAC by source;
10. normalized replacement cost of founder/volunteer functions when material.

These are not all blockers at once. Resolve them when the current operational block needs them.

---

# 5. What not to do yet

Do not make the next phase primarily about:

- cold paid ads;
- broad content calendar;
- large nurture automation;
- digital product build;
- B2B expansion;
- complex lifecycle catalog;
- sophisticated CRM fields without an operating use.

Those become more valuable after the Full can be sold and fulfilled cleanly.

---

# 6. Phase scorecard

Operationalization is judged by execution readiness, not document count.

Minimum checks:

- customer can complete the journey without ad-hoc founder invention;
- every promised component has an owner and fulfilment path;
- material exceptions have a defined action;
- customer protection can actually be honored;
- Pack/Guide/refresh promises are deliverable;
- key customer/proof/economic events are capturable;
- first active-demand sale can be attributed and fulfilled;
- unresolved clinical/legal QA remains visible and cannot be mistaken for approval.

---

# 7. Revision condition

Change this order only if a real operational dependency blocks the current item or new evidence shows another missing prerequisite prevents a sale from being fulfilled/measured.

Do not reorder because another asset is more interesting to build.
