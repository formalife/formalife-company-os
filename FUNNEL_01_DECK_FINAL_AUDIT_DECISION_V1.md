# 3. Surgical production revisions

## DECISION 5 — S01 anti-panic boundary is verbal, not another slide

Keep S01 visual core:

**`RICONOSCERE. DECIDERE. AGIRE.`**

Opening spoken framing must make clear that `Anti-Panico` is not a promise of guaranteed calm or absence of fear. The product meaning is operational: do not have to invent the response from zero when something changes.

Do not add a disclaimer wall to S01.

## DECISION 6 — S06 uses one dominant number

Primary visual evidence:

**`12,6%`** — current ISS maternal-report figure with correct population/source.

The historical `~50 accessi PS / 1.000 bambini 0–4 / anno` may remain verbally subordinate or in notes/reference, but must not compete as a second hero number.

## DECISION 7 — S09 headline/copy is shortened

Working production direction:

**`IL CIBO È COINVOLTO NELLA MAGGIOR PARTE DEGLI EPISODI RIPORTATI`**

`60–80%` remains the protagonist visual. Source, endpoint and caveat belong in footer/notes and instructor wording, not inside an institutional headline.

## DECISION 8 — S10 has one primary measure

Primary projected measure:

**`~25–27% delle morti accidentali 0–4`** with explicit population/source framing.

`~40–50 decessi/anno 0–14` becomes visually secondary or spoken.

Remove the optional age-gradient build from the main deck unless clinical review identifies a specific learning job that requires it.

## DECISION 9 — S13 makes FERMA operational, not calming

Working headline direction:

**`FERMA: 2–3 SECONDI PER ORIENTARTI`**

Instructor wording must state explicitly that FERMA is a short operational reset, not a prolonged attempt to calm down before acting.

## DECISION 10 — S20 reverses the visual hierarchy

Primary participant-facing decision objects become:

**`TOSSE EFFICACE`** vs **`TOSSE INEFFICACE / ASSENTE`**.

`ostruzione parziale` and `ostruzione severa/totale` remain the subordinate conceptual explanation.

The teaching sequence remains:

**mechanism -> observable cough/airflow state -> decision -> action.**

## DECISION 11 — S27 gains a private instructor progression checklist

Do not add more participant-facing content to S27.

Create/use a private instructor micro-checklist to decide whether a learner is ready to move from corrected attempt to scenario/no-prompt state. It must cover, subject to clinical validation:

- recognition of the relevant state;
- safe position/support;
- critical manoeuvre mechanics;
- reassessment / state-change recognition;
- escalation / next-state logic;
- absence of material unsafe/prohibited action.

The checklist is a delivery-control tool, not a public score or certification.

## DECISION 12 — S41 adds a boundary retrieval

After the QCPR feedback debrief, ask:

**`Questo grafico ci ha aiutato. Che cosa NON ci sta misurando?`**

Expected recovery includes the missing whole-sequence jobs, subject to approved wording:

- ventilations;
- recognition / entry into the algorithm;
- 112 role;
- AED role;
- the complete pediatric sequence.

Purpose: prevent the measurable compression trace from becoming the learner's mental model of all pediatric CPR.

## DECISION 13 — S44 / ACT 5 adds four micro-retrievals maximum

S44 must explicitly frame ACT 5 as:

**first-action orientation, not eight mini-courses.**

Add at most four 5–10 second retrieval prompts across ACT 5, without new physical slides and without opening discussion blocks.

Current production set:

2. anaphylaxis -> what must not be awaited;
3. drowning/post-removal -> what changes if not breathing normally;
4. severe bleeding -> first priority.

Exact prompts/answers remain subject to clinical sign-off.
