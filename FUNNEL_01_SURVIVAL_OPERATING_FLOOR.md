# Funnel 01 — Survival Operating Floor

Status: CURRENT FOUNDER DECISION — temporary survival-phase operating rule
Date: 2026-09-22

Purpose: record the founder-approved minimum operating threshold for current B2C Full editions during the cash-constrained survival phase, while preserving the distinction between short-term cash generation and a sustainable normalized business model.

This decision supersedes earlier provisional 8- or 10-participant activation floors wherever they conflict.

---

## 1. Founder decision

For the current survival phase, Formalife may confirm and deliver a B2C Full edition with a minimum of:

**5 paying participants.**

Rationale supplied by founder:

- the company currently needs to generate cash even from sub-scale editions;
- the current instructors are Formalife shareholders and are not currently paid cash compensation for delivery;
- room cost in Brescia remains approximately EUR 60;
- the founder explicitly recognizes that unpaid shareholder delivery is **not a sustainable long-term business model**;
- the temporary objective is to keep the company moving while the commercial engine is being rebuilt.

This is therefore a **survival/cash-flow decision**, not evidence that a 5-participant edition has acceptable normalized economics.

---

## 2. Two economic views must remain separate

### A. Current cash-survival view

For the current survival decision, instructor cash outflow is treated as EUR 0 because shareholders deliver the course without current compensation.

Using current planning contribution before edition fixed cost:

- 1 Caregiver order contribution before room/CAC: ~EUR 54.42;
- 2 Caregivers order contribution before room/CAC: ~EUR 81.66 per two occupied seats;
- Brescia room cash cost: EUR 60.

Illustrative 5-participant editions before CAC/overhead:

| 5-participant mix | Cash contribution after room, before CAC | Normalized contribution after room + EUR 200 instructor cost |
|---|---:|---:|
| 5 x 1 Caregiver | ~EUR 212.10 | ~EUR 12.10 |
| 3 x 1 Caregiver + 1 x 2 Caregivers | ~EUR 184.92 | ~EUR -15.08 |
| 1 x 1 Caregiver + 2 x 2 Caregivers | ~EUR 157.74 | ~EUR -42.26 |

Therefore a 5-person edition can generate positive short-term cash before CAC while still being economically weak or negative under normalized instructor economics.

### B. Normalized business-model view

For strategic sustainability and scaling, continue to reserve:

- EUR 120 primary instructor;
- EUR 80 secondary instructor;
- EUR 200 total normalized instructor cost / edition.

Do **not** use current unpaid shareholder labor to conclude that the Full is sustainably profitable at five participants.

---

## 3. Customer-facing activation rule

For selected `EDIZIONE IN ATTIVAZIONE — PRE-ISCRIZIONE PROTETTA` campaigns, the current default minimum can therefore be communicated as:

**the edition is confirmed when at least 5 paying participants are reached by the published confirmation deadline.**

The exact threshold must still be visible before pre-enrolment and must not be changed retroactively for that edition.

Formalife may voluntarily confirm an edition above or below another threshold only when the published customer commitment remains honored; do not advertise one threshold and operate another invisibly.

---

## 4. Guardrails

The 5-participant floor does not authorize blind paid acquisition.

For every edition still read:

- source;
- CAC if any;
- 1/2-Caregiver mix;
- cash contribution;
- normalized contribution;
- occupied seat count;
- refunds/transfers/guarantee exposure;
- founder/instructor capacity.

If paid acquisition makes a 5-person edition cash-negative, the existence of a 5-person operational floor does not make that campaign economically valid.

---

## 5. Revision trigger

This temporary floor must be revisited when one or more of the following become true:

- instructors begin receiving actual cash compensation;
- CAC becomes material/readable;
- editions routinely fill beyond five participants;
- room/travel/pack costs change materially;
- shareholder delivery capacity becomes a bottleneck;
- company cash position no longer requires survival-mode decisions.

The target remains to move toward a model that works on normalized economics rather than founder subsidy.
