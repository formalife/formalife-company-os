# Funnel 01 — ACT 7 Conclusion / Continuity Run-of-Show V1

Status: CURRENT FOUNDER-APPROVED OPERATING RUN-OF-SHOW — referral mechanics governed by `FUNNEL_01_REFERRAL_SYSTEM_V2.md`; exact medical wording inside final retrieval remains subject to Dott.ssa Mafalda Camposarcone sign-off
Date: 2026-09-22

Purpose: close the live Full Course in a way that preserves the learning peak created by ACT 6, compresses critical memory, transfers one concrete action into the home, explains the AFTER system without turning the ending into a sales/admin dump, and finishes on a meaningful bookend to ACT 0.

Parent architecture:

- `FUNNEL_01_G3_FULL_OFFER_ARCHITECTURE.md`

Layer 1 governing direction:

**promise -> delivery -> result/experience -> satisfaction/proof -> referral/retention.**

Relevant specialist referral principle:

**satisfaction real -> appropriate moment -> explicit request -> facilitated introduction -> sustainable incentive.**

Research basis checked 2026-09-22:

- retrieval practice supports longer-term retention, including in health-professions education;
- implementation intentions are more effective when plans are specific/contingent and rehearsed;
- endings can disproportionately influence retrospective experience, but this is treated as a design consideration rather than a universal law.

---

# 1. Core design decision

ACT 7 should **not** attempt to create a second dramatic peak after the Complex Mirror Test.

ACT 6 is the experiential peak.

ACT 7 has four jobs:

1. **compress** the course into a few retrievable anchors;
2. **transfer** one concrete action into the participant's next 24 hours;
3. **hand off** the customer into Guide / Pack / customer stream / refresh / referral;
4. **bookend** ACT 0 with a short final meaning close after administration is already complete.

The actual last memory of the official event should therefore **not** be:

- signing a form;
- collecting a certificate;
- listening to a referral pitch;
- asking where the toilets/parking are;
- waiting in a queue for materials.

Operational/admin work is deliberately moved **before the final 2 min 15 sec close**.

# 3. 00:00–00:01:15 — Final memory compression

## Job

Prevent the final ten minutes from adding more information.

ACT 6 has already tested transfer. ACT 7 now compresses the entire course into the smallest set of anchors worth carrying out of the room.

No new slide deck.

Preferred visual: one slide only.

### Anchor 1 — Mental orientation

**FERMA -> VALUTA -> AGISCI**

Do not reteach its internal structure here.

### Anchor 2 — Choking state change

**effective cough / airflow -> monitor and do not interfere**

versus

**ineffective/absent cough -> severe obstruction response + help**

Exact customer wording remains subject to Mafalda sign-off.

### Anchor 3 — If the child becomes unresponsive

Current working consumer sequence:

**5 initial rescue breaths -> 30:2 -> AED as soon as available -> continue according to 112/AED instructions.**

Exact medical wording remains governed by ACT 4 sign-off.

### Facilitation

Instructor should retrieve, not lecture.

Fast prompts:

- `Le tre parole?`
- `Qual è il cambio che cerchiamo nel soffocamento?`
- `Se perde coscienza e non respira normalmente, qual è la sequenza che deve tornarvi in mente?`

Room answers together.

Then stop.

Do not summarize all ACT 5 emergencies again.

# 4. 00:01:15–00:02:15 — 24-hour transfer commitment

## Founder decision

Every participant chooses **one concrete household action to complete within 24 hours**.

Purpose:

move one learning outcome from `I understood it in class` to `I changed something in my household`.

Do not give everyone the same homework.

Suggested options:

- agree with another caregiver who acts and who calls 112;
- review the preparation of one or more foods currently common at home;
- inspect one room for small objects / button batteries / avoidable hazards;
- show the Family Emergency Pack / emergency contacts to a grandparent, babysitter or other caregiver;
- choose another specific action that emerged from the course.

Preferred implementation-intention wording:

**`Entro domani, dopo __________, farò __________.`**

Example:

`Dopo cena, io e il mio partner decidiamo chi chiama il 112 e chi interviene.`

## Physical implementation — founder-approved direction

Do not add a large new product component.

V1 implementation:

1. add a `La mia azione nelle prossime 24 ore` field to the existing Family Safety Checklist;
2. if two caregivers need separate ownership, duplicate a very small personal slip/card only where useful;
3. use an existing participant worksheet if one is later produced and makes the duplicate piece unnecessary.

Participants take ~20–30 seconds to write the action.

No one has to read it aloud.

# 5. 00:02:15–00:04:30 — AFTER system handoff

## Job

Make visible that the live event is not the whole preparedness system.

Do not enumerate every object in the Pack.

Use a four-part model:

### 1. GUIDE — `capire / ripassare il soffocamento`

The Guide is the durable choking-specific reference.

### 2. FAMILY EMERGENCY PACK — `portare il sistema a casa`

Show only the key jobs:

- emergency/reference material;
- family/caregiver handover;
- safety checklist;
- scenario/rehearsal tools;
- QR to current versioned resources.

Important boundary:

Pack cards support preparation/rehearsal/reference; they are not positioned as a substitute for direct recall during time-critical CPR/choking actions.

### 3. CUSTOMER UPDATE STREAM — `mantenere il sistema aggiornato`

Explain briefly that Formalife will continue to send useful/verified pediatric-safety information according to the customer's communication permissions/preferences.

Do not turn this into `iscrivetevi alla newsletter` after the customer already bought.

### 4. FREE PRACTICAL REFRESH — `tornare a fare pratica`

Every Full participant has one free practical retraining opportunity within 12 months, subject to current booking/capacity rules.

Customer-facing job here:

**remember that the entitlement exists and know where to book it.**

Do not explain all capacity exceptions verbally; they live in the written/digital service terms.

# 6. 00:04:30–00:05:30 — Pass Sicurezza referral invitation

This is a current founder-approved referral mechanic.

Each household Pack contains **2 Pass Sicurezza**.

Keep the ask short and concrete.

Current direction:

**`Nel vostro Pack trovate due Pass Sicurezza. Se vi viene in mente una persona che si occupa regolarmente di un bambino e a cui pensate possa essere utile prepararsi meglio, passategliene uno. Non dovete spiegarle o venderle il corso: il Pass la porta nel punto giusto per capire da sola se vuole vedere direttamente date e programma oppure prima approfondire.`**

Then explain the reward in one sentence:

**`Se una persona che avete presentato acquista poi un Full, vi riconosciamo una Guida Anti-Panico gratuita da regalare a un'altra famiglia o caregiver.`**

Do not add:

- urgency gimmicks;
- a request for names/phone numbers in the room;
- a second referral pitch after the final close;
- review request at T=0.

The Google-review request remains T+24h.

The T+7 digital referral request remains only for actual reviewers/testimonial givers under `FUNNEL_01_REFERRAL_SYSTEM_V2.md`.

# 7. 00:05:30–00:07:45 — Certificates / materials / operational handoff

## Founder decision

Administration happens **before** the official emotional ending.

### Instructor B

During the preceding continuity explanation, Instructor B should already have materials ordered by participant/household.

At this stage complete:

- certificate handoff;
- any remaining Guide/Pack handoff;
- attendance/document confirmation;
- ensure QR/access items are present;
- resolve obvious missing-material issues quickly.

### Instructor A

Use the time only for concise practical orientation:

- where refresh booking is found;
- where current resources are found;
- support/contact route if something is missing.

No open-ended Q&A here.

## Operational goal

By 00:07:45:

- every participant has their materials/documents;
- no signatures or certificate queue remains;
- bags can stay closed;
- the room can give full attention to the final close.

If a venue/process currently requires signatures at the exit desk, redesign that process rather than accepting admin as the final event memory.

---

# 8. 00:07:45–00:10:00 — Final bookend / official close

## Founder-approved direction

Close the meaning of the day, not its logistics.

The ending reconnects four things:

1. the uncertainty visible in ACT 0;
2. the materially harder scenario handled in ACT 6;
3. the participant's one 24-hour household action;
4. the fact that preparation continues after the room.

## Choreography

### Step 1 — remove operational visuals

No QR codes.
No bullet list of benefits.
No referral slide.
No certificate slide.

Preferred final visual:

- a normal family moment / ordinary meal / ordinary day;
- or a very clean neutral slide with one short line.

Do not finish on an emergency image.

### Step 2 — one authentic reflection

If the ACT 0 volunteer also completed ACT 6 and is comfortable, ask only:

**`Rispetto all'inizio, qual è la cosa che adesso ti sembra più chiara quando una situazione cambia?`**

Allow a short authentic answer.

Do not turn it into a testimonial request.

If a different volunteer was used or the participant is uncomfortable, skip this step rather than forcing symmetry.

### Step 3 — reconnect the room

Current approved direction:

**`All'inizio abbiamo visto quanto sia facile partire dall'istinto quando non abbiamo ancora una struttura. Alla fine avete visto una situazione più difficile, con più cose da riconoscere e più decisioni da prendere. Il punto non è uscire da qui pensando di sapere tutto. Il punto è non dover partire da zero.`**

### Step 4 — signature close V1

Founder-approved V1 meaning:

**`Prepararsi non significa vivere aspettando che succeda un'emergenza. Significa poter vivere le giornate normali sapendo che, se qualcosa cambia, non dovrete inventare tutto da zero.`**

Then, after a brief pause:

**`Avete un modo per fermarvi, valutare, agire — e strumenti per continuare ad allenarlo. Questo era l'obiettivo di oggi.`**

Thank the room.

Course officially ends.

The exact spoken copy may later be polished for natural delivery, but the **meaning/order is current founder decision** and should not be replaced with generic motivational language.

Do not claim:

- that participants are now clinically competent;
- that they will remain calm;
- that they can handle every pediatric emergency;
- that the course guarantees an outcome.

# 9. What happens after the official close

The official event is over.

Instructors may remain for:

- individual non-diagnostic clarifications;
- logistics;
- optional photo where consented;
- missing material/support issues.

Founder decision:

**do not restart a group Q&A, sales pitch or Google-review request after the official close.**

Google review remains T+24h.

- Pass ask;

## Instructor B — operational owner

- prepare/distribute materials;

- keep the room ready for the final attention reset.

At the final 2:15 both instructors stop operational activity and face the room.

# 12. Rehearsal scorecard

Record:

## Timing

- total ACT 7 duration;
- admin duration;
- whether final close still has a full uninterrupted 2 min 15 sec;
- whether any material queue continues after official end.

## Learning

- room can retrieve the three anchors without prompting overload;
- participants write a specific 24-hour action rather than a vague intention;
- Pack/refresh purpose is understood without long explanation.

## Experience

- referral ask feels like facilitated sharing rather than a sales pitch;
- operational handoff does not fragment the room;
- attention visibly returns for the final close;
- final close feels like a bookend to ACT 0/6 rather than generic motivation.

## Continuity

Check later:

- percentage of attendees reporting/completing their 24-hour action if measured lightly;
- refresh awareness/redemption;
- Pass usage;
- support questions that reveal Pack confusion.

Do not add a post-course survey item unless it creates decision-useful evidence.

---

# 13. Failure modes

ACT 7 fails if:

- final ten minutes become a product inventory;
- the Pack is pitched as a pile of bonuses;
- participants queue for documents after the final line;
- referral becomes the emotional endpoint;
- the instructor starts a new Q&A block at minute 250;
- the close overstates confidence/clinical competence;
- the emotional close is generic enough to fit any parenting seminar;
- the 24-hour action is vague (`stare più attento`) rather than observable;
- participants leave unsure how to access the refresh.
