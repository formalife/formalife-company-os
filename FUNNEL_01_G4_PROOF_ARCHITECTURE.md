# Funnel 01 — G4 Proof Architecture

Status: CURRENT OPERATING ARCHITECTURE — proof must be earned progressively; customer-facing claims remain bounded by actual evidence
Date: 2026-09-22

Purpose: define how Funnel 01 makes its important claims believable without using generic social proof, unsupported superiority language or historical evidence beyond what it actually proves.

G4 follows the founder-approved G2 positioning and G3 Full offer architecture.

Core doctrine:

**claim -> specific perceived risk/question -> appropriate proof -> consequence for the buyer -> next action.**

A pile of reviews is not a proof strategy.

---

# 1. Governing proof rules

## Rule 1 — Proof must match the claim

A review saying “bravi e gentili” can support general satisfaction. It cannot prove:

- scientific quality;
- superior practical learning;
- usefulness of the new Family Emergency Pack;
- value of the new broader Full;
- effectiveness of the refresh system;
- clinical outcomes.

## Rule 2 — Product provenance must stay visible

Historical proof from the previous choking-focused course may support:

- Formalife has delivered real parent courses;
- participants experienced real live teaching/practice;
- historical customers were satisfied where the review actually says so.

It must **not** be presented as proof that the materially rebuilt broader Full already delivered its new before/live/after preparedness-system promise.

## Rule 3 — Process evidence can prove process; outcome evidence must prove outcome

Examples:

- photo/video of supervised practice -> proves supervised practice occurred;
- curriculum / Pack preview -> proves components exist;
- refund record -> proves a guarantee was honored;
- customer statement -> proves that customer's reported experience;
- none of these alone proves superior real-emergency performance.

## Rule 4 — Authority, credibility and trust are different jobs

Formalife may use scientific/human authority before the sale, but authority does not replace proof of the actual customer experience.

## Rule 5 — Do not wait until the bottom of the page to prove everything

Place proof near the question/objection it resolves.

## Rule 6 — First rebuilt editions are also evidence-production events

The rebuilt Full must deliberately create new, current proof as it is delivered.

Do not rely indefinitely on historical choking-course proof.

# 2. Proof strength states

Each material proof job should be tracked internally with one of these evidence states:

- `P0_NONE` — assertion only;
- `P1_PROCESS` — the process/component visibly exists;
- `P2_HISTORICAL` — relevant historical Formalife evidence, but from prior product/version;
- `P3_CURRENT_SINGLE` — one current rebuilt-Full case/customer;
- `P4_CURRENT_REPEATED` — repeated current evidence across multiple customers/editions;
- `P5_QUANTIFIED` — measured current pattern with denominator/method/time period retained.

Do not turn `P2_HISTORICAL` into `P4_CURRENT_REPEATED` through copywriting.

Do not turn self-reported confidence into a claim of clinical competence.

# 5. Claim boundaries — prohibited or restricted until evidence exists

Do not use as Formalife conclusions without evidence sufficient to the exact wording:

- “il migliore corso”;
- “il più completo”;
- “unico in Italia”;
- “scientificamente superiore”;
- “clinicamente provato”;
- “ti farà mantenere la calma”;
- “eliminerà il panico”;
- “ricorderai sicuramente cosa fare sotto stress”;
- “saprai salvare una vita” as a guaranteed result;
- “riduce mortalità / complicanze” as a Formalife course outcome;
- “dopo il corso saprai intervenire correttamente in qualunque emergenza”;
- comparative superiority versus named competitors without matched evidence.

Permissible direction is narrower and truthful:

- what the course teaches;
- what participants practice;
- what is included;
- who reviews the medical/scientific content;
- what policies Formalife commits to;
- what real customers specifically report about their experience.

Every medical/scientific claim remains subject to Mafalda's specific review before customer-facing use.

# 6. Proof architecture by funnel state

## A. Latent / low-awareness prospect

Primary proof job:

**“This subject is worth listening to and the source is credible.”**

Use primarily:

- Mafalda's relevant pediatric authority;
- useful published educational material;
- Guide/book authority;
- carefully sourced/validated problem education.

Do not overload this stage with testimonials about buying a course before the person accepts the problem/category.

## B. Problem-aware prospect

Primary proof job:

**“Formalife understands this exact parent concern and provides useful, credible education.”**

Use:

- specific choking/weaning educational proof;
- parent-affinity historical/current testimonials where relevant;
- Guide previews;
- authority close to medical statements.

## C. Solution-aware prospect

Primary proof job:

**“There are meaningful differences between passive information, reference material and supervised practical preparation.”**

Use:

- visible process/curriculum evidence;
- authentic practice footage;
- explanation of live vs reference roles;
- testimonials specifically describing practice/correction, not generic satisfaction.

## D. Product-aware prospect

Primary proof job:

**“The Formalife package is real, coherent and worth considering at this price.”**

Use:

- before/live/after package visual;
- Guide and Pack preview;
- Mafalda role;
- rebuilt-Full testimonials as soon as available;
- guarantee/Total Protection;
- 1/2 Caregiver explanation;
- refresh entitlement;
- exact scope and exclusions.

## E. Brand-aware / trusted referral / high intent

Primary proof job:

**remove final decision risk without creating unnecessary funnel depth.**

Use near CTA:

- relevant current testimonials;
- package proof;
- authority;
- dates/real seat availability;
- Total Protection;
- BTRF guarantee;
- exact price/what is included.

## F. Checkout / abandoned checkout

Proof must answer the observed barrier, not replay the whole persuasion sequence.

Examples:

- trust -> authority/customer proof;
- value -> package + relevant testimonial;
- scheduling -> Total Protection / waitlist;
- fear of choosing wrong -> BTRF + fit/non-fit;
- “does credit work?” -> exact Training Credit mechanics.

# 7. Proof capture system for every rebuilt Full edition

The first rebuilt editions must deliberately generate current evidence.

## BEFORE purchase / registration — buyer context

Where friction remains low, capture:

1. what happened now that made the topic relevant;
2. whether the person was already looking for a course;
3. alternatives considered / what they had used before;
4. why Formalife entered consideration;
5. what almost stopped the purchase;
6. why 1 Caregiver or 2 Caregivers was chosen.

Purpose:

- positioning evidence;
- offer evidence;
- future testimonial context;
- no-sale diagnosis.

Do not ask every field if it reduces conversion without changing a decision.

## DURING delivery — process evidence

For each edition retain simple operating evidence:

- date/version of curriculum;
- instructors present;
- participant count;
- 1/2 Caregiver mix;
- delivery checklist completed;
- which practical stations were delivered;
- authentic current photos/video where permission exists;
- any material delivery deviation/service issue.

This is primarily internal proof-of-delivery and future visual proof.

## IMMEDIATELY AFTER — experience evidence

Ask all participants concise questions such as:

1. What brought you to the course now?
2. What did you expect before arriving?
3. What part was most useful in practice?
4. Was there anything materially different from what you expected from a normal choking/first-aid course?
5. Did the broader emergency-preparedness scope make sense after entering through choking/svezzamento?
6. What did supervised practice/correction add for you?
7. What will you actually use after today — Guide, Pack, framework, refresh?
8. What was unclear, weak or disappointing?
9. Who would you recommend this to, if anyone?

Do not supply the desired Formalife phrasing inside the question.

## 24H / short follow-up — offer + guarantee proof

Capture:

- unresolved service problem;
- guarantee request if any;
- what the participant remembers as the main value;
- whether Pack/reference access worked.

Service recovery takes precedence over testimonial collection.

## LATER follow-up — durable-use evidence

Appropriate future checks can ask:

- whether reference material was consulted;
- whether another caregiver used/read/shared it;
- whether customer booked the free refresh;
- what trigger created a new safety need;
- whether the customer referred another family.

Do not convert non-response into evidence of success.

# 8. Testimonial production architecture

Layer 1 requires testimonials to be designed for a proof job without scripting false words.

Build a library tagged by:

- product/version;
- date;
- target/context;
- original trigger;
- awareness state if known;
- objection/risk answered;
- proof job;
- media: text/video/audio;
- permission state;
- historical vs rebuilt Full.

High-value testimonial types for Funnel 01:

### T1 — “I came because of choking / weaning”

Proves relevance of beachhead trigger.

### T2 — “I expected only choking; the broader preparedness system mattered”

Supports G2/G3 bridge from focused entry to broader Full.

### T3 — practical correction

Specific account of why doing the maneuver and receiving correction was useful.

### T4 — decision clarity

Specific account of clearer first-response/escalation reasoning, without claiming guaranteed real-world performance.

### T5 — 2-Caregiver household value

Why preparing two regular caregivers mattered.

### T6 — durable reference

How Guide/Pack was used after the course.

### T7 — customer protection

Real experience of a transfer, refund, activation refund or guarantee being honored.

### T8 — refresh

Real experience of returning within 12 months and finding the practice refresh useful.

### T9 — anti-procrastination

Only where true: a customer explains why they wish they had prepared earlier and what real inconvenience/uncertainty was associated with waiting.

Do not manufacture fear or invent consequences.

Formalife should avoid making testimonials carry every burden.

Use a portfolio:

## Authority proof

- Mafalda bio/photo/role;
- Guide/publication;
- expert educational content;
- relevant external appearances/partnerships when real.

## Product proof

- current curriculum map;
- real Guide;
- real Pack;
- authentic course photos/video;
- before/live/after visual.

## Process proof

- practical stations;
- instructor correction;
- versioned materials;
- refresh booking mechanism;
- guarantee/refund process.

## Customer proof

- testimonials;
- reviews;
- referrals;
- repeat/next purchases when relevant.

## Quantified proof — later

Only after enough volume and correct denominators:

- % choosing 2 Caregivers;
- % reporting specific value attributes;
- refresh redemption rate;
- referral rate;
- guarantee claim rate;
- Pack/reference usage;
- Guide -> Full conversion;
- reasons for choosing Formalife.

Always preserve sample, period and question/method behind any percentage.

# 12. Historical proof migration plan

Until enough rebuilt-Full proof exists:

### Allowed

Use historical choking-course proof with truthful context such as:

- previous Formalife live-course participants;
- prior experience with supervised choking practice;
- historical customer satisfaction where stated.

### Required transition

As rebuilt Full editions run:

1. collect new current evidence;
2. tag it as rebuilt Full;
3. replace historical proof near claims specific to the new Full;
4. retain historical proof only where it remains relevant to operating history or matching experience.

### Do not do

Do not redesign historical testimonials visually in a way that makes them appear to come from the current Full if they do not.

# 14. G4 launch gate vs scale gate

## G4-L — enough proof to launch controlled rebuilt-Full tests

Before first controlled rebuilt-Full commercial test, Formalife needs:

1. truthful Mafalda authority presentation;
2. reviewed customer-facing medical/scientific claims;
3. current accurate scope/package visuals;
4. authentic historical proof clearly labelled/provenanced where used;
5. current practice/delivery imagery if available, otherwise process proof that can be produced on the first edition;
6. exact guarantee/Total Protection terms implemented;
7. no unsupported superiority/outcome claims.

The first edition does **not** require a mature library of rebuilt-Full testimonials; logically, those do not yet exist.

## G4-S — enough proof to scale cold demand aggressively

Before broad cold scaling, stronger evidence is required:

1. repeated current rebuilt-Full customer proof;
2. proof that choking-led entrants understand the broader preparedness meaning;
3. evidence on which offer elements actually influence choice/value;
4. current practical-delivery proof;
5. customer-protection policies demonstrably honored;
6. proof coverage across main objections, not only generic satisfaction;
7. quantified claims only where denominator/method are retained;
8. conversion/contribution/CAC/payback compatible with the proof-led offer.

A beautiful testimonial library does not justify scaling an economically broken funnel.

# 16. G4 pass / revision criteria

G4 is functioning when:

- every material claim has an identifiable proof source or is removed/narrowed;
- proof appears where the buyer needs it;
- historical and current-product proof are not confused;
- scientific authority is visible without substituting for product proof;
- testimonials are specific to real objections/results rather than generic praise;
- the rebuilt Full continuously creates new proof;
- service failures trigger recovery before advocacy requests;
- no marketing claim outruns delivery reality;
- proof production remains operationally light enough to happen every edition;
- proof improves conversion/trust without creating unmanageable cost or compliance risk.

Revise if the system becomes a bureaucratic archive rather than a selling/customer-learning asset.
